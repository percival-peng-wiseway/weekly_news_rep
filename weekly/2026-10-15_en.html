Policy & Regulation

Standards Australia sets out voluntary guidance for flexible loads
Source: Standards Australia | Date: 2026-10-15
URL：Original article
Key point: Standards Australia’s 15 October announcement describes a voluntary Flexible Loads Interoperability Guide covering EV chargers, hot-water systems, air conditioning and pool pumps. Its informative control hierarchy prioritises safety, available consumer manual overrides and local network constraints ahead of external retailer or aggregator instructions. The guide supports consumer-energy-resource integration and future standards development; it is not a new mandatory regulation.

Solar

ARENA launches up to A$250 million Solar Sunshot manufacturing round
Source: ARENA | Date: 2026-10-15
URL：Original article
Key point: ARENA launched Solar Sunshot Round 3 on 15 October, offering up to A$250 million for commercial-scale Australian manufacturing of solar balance-of-plant and deployment technologies, including racking, tracking, robotics and prefabrication. Australian and international companies may propose local manufacturing or product development, with expressions of interest opening on 2 November and an information webinar on 26 October. This is a new funding round, separate from the earlier robotics awards.

Battery Storage (BESS)

ArcActive’s lead-acid storage pilot operates at Deakin in Geelong
Source: Energy Source & Distribution | Date: 2026-10-15
URL：Original article
Key point: Reporting published on 15 October describes an operating ArcActive pilot at Deakin University’s Waurn Ponds Renewable Grid-Connected Battery Testing Facility, supported by Origin Energy. It uses redesigned lead-acid technology targeting commercial and industrial energy storage. The report does not establish a 15 October commissioning date or provide system capacity or measured bill savings, so its cost-reduction objectives remain unproven in the reported results.

Wind

Warracknabeal clears Victorian approvals; federal decision remains pending
Source: ABC News | Date: 2026-10-15
URL：Original article
Key point: ABC reported on 15 October that Victoria’s planning minister signed the final state approval for WestWind’s Warracknabeal Energy Park earlier that week. The proposal comprises 219 wind turbines, 1.5 GW of generation and two 300 MW battery systems. Federal approval remains outstanding, with the assessment decision temporarily paused while the developer supplies further information; this is not a construction-start announcement.
Cubico signs conditional community-benefit deals for two Queensland projects
Source: RenewEconomy / Western Downs Regional Council | Date: 2026-10-15
URL：Original article
Key point: A 15 October report covers community-benefit agreements for Cubico’s Marmadua and Middle Creek projects, both dated 14 October in Western Downs Regional Council’s register. Payments could total up to A$160 million over 30 years with inflation indexation, subject to project approvals and final scale. Sponsorship and education payments begin after approval, while infrastructure funding begins at construction; the agreements do not resolve the projects’ outstanding planning processes.

Company & Project Developments

Renewables reach a record 5 GW of investment decisions in nine months
Source: DCCEEW / Chris Bowen | Date: 2026-10-15
URL：Original article
Key point: A 15 October ministerial release citing Clean Energy Regulator data reports 5 GW of large-scale renewable final investment decisions (FID) in January–September 2026, above the previous full-year record of 4.9 GW. The September quarter contributed 2.1 GW, comprising 1.1 GW of wind and 1 GW of solar; solar FID reached 3.5 GW year to date. These are investment decisions, not newly commissioned generating capacity.

Infrastructure & Transmission

New England REZ transmission environmental statement opens for comment
Source: EnergyCo NSW | Date: 2026-10-15
URL：Original article
Key point: EnergyCo opened public exhibition of the New England REZ transmission project’s environmental impact statement on 15 October, with submissions closing on 12 November. The proposal includes dual 500 kV lines linking to Bayswater near Muswellbrook, with initial network capacity of 6 GW across two stages and potential expansion to 8 GW by 2034. This is an assessment and consultation milestone, not project approval or the start of construction.
ACCIONA selected for Gippsland offshore-wind transmission development
Source: VicGrid | Date: 2026-10-15
URL：Original article
Key point: VicGrid’s 15 October update names ACCIONA as preferred development partner for transmission to connect the first 2 GW of Gippsland offshore wind. The project is expected to be completed in 2031, but remains subject to environmental, planning and other statutory approvals. A final construction offer follows the development phase, so the appointment is not an unconditional construction award.
Clean Energy Link–East contract award targets access for 2.3 GW of renewables
Source: pv magazine Australia / WA Government | Date: 2026-10-15
URL：Original article
Key point: Reporting on 15 October covers WA’s 14 October award of Clean Energy Link–East work to ACCIONA, GenusPlus and UGL. Stage one includes a new 330 kV Cordering terminal, expansions at Shotts and Muja, and respective 38 km and 54 km transmission connections. The government expects the completed project to enable approximately 2.3 GW of renewable capacity; this is a contract milestone, not operating infrastructure.
