Policy & Regulation

Opposition proposes scrapping ARENA and home battery subsidies
Source: AAP / RenewEconomy | Date: 2026-10-26
URL: Original source
Key point: AAP reported on 26 October that Coalition leader Angus Taylor proposed abolishing ARENA, the Net Zero Economy Agency and home battery and electric vehicle subsidies under a future Coalition government, as part of funding promised tax cuts. These are opposition proposals, not enacted changes or the withdrawal of current entitlements.

Solar

Array partners with Aeeris on automated hail response for Australian solar
Source: pv magazine Australia / Early Warning Network | Date: 2026-10-26
URL: Original source · Primary supporting source
Key point: On 26 October, pv magazine Australia reported that Aeeris subsidiary Early Warning Network had partnered with Array to integrate Australian hail detection and forecasts into SmarTrack, enabling automatic tracker positioning ahead of severe weather. The company release confirms the Australian integration, but does not identify newly deployed project capacity or installation numbers. Reduced damage and improved insurability or bankability are company expectations, not independently verified performance or a guarantee against hail losses.

Solar & BESS Investment

CEC reports A$32.5 billion of generation and storage financed or under construction
Source: Clean Energy Council | Date: 2026-10-26
URL: Original source
Key point: The CEC’s 26 October investment report says 12 large-scale renewable projects totalling 2.6 GW reached financial close in the first half of 2026, including 11 solar or solar-hybrid projects. Nine large batteries entered operation, adding 2.3 GW/7.1 GWh. Across generation and storage, 141 projects worth about A$32.5 billion were financed or under construction. These are distinct development stages: the whole investment pipeline is not operating capacity.

Wind

Construction starts on 470 MW Parron Maam Marang wind farm
Source: RenewEconomy / Atmos Renewables | Date: 2026-10-26
URL: Original source · Primary supporting source
Key point: RenewEconomy reported on 26 October that construction had started on WA’s 470 MW Parron Maam Marang wind farm. Atmos’s primary release dates the sod-turn and construction start to 23 October. Backed by a long-term Synergy power agreement, the project targets operation by the end of 2028, around 400 peak construction jobs and an annual A$470,000 community fund. This is a construction milestone, not commissioning; timing and jobs remain developer projections.

Companies & Projects

SP Energy outlines staged 4 GW Pilbara wind-and-solar proposal
Source: RenewEconomy / SP Energy | Date: 2026-10-26
URL: Original source · Primary supporting source
Key point: RenewEconomy reported on 26 October that SP Energy proposes the 4 GW Wallareenya wind-and-solar development south of Port Hedland, serving green iron and other industrial customers. The developer targets construction of an initial 1 GW stage, including transmission, in 2028; later stages depend on demand, infrastructure and customer agreements. Early investigations and land discussions are under way, but full planning applications and financing remain outstanding. It is not a fully approved or construction-ready project.

Grid & Related Infrastructure

Unmatched data centre load could add A$40 billion in wholesale costs: SEC scenario
Source: Smart Energy Council / RenewEconomy | Date: 2026-10-26
URL: Original source · Primary supporting source
Key point: The Smart Energy Council analysis published on 26 October tests an additional 5 GW of data centre load from July 2028, above AEMO forecasts, before matching new supply catches up. It estimates A$40 billion in extra wholesale electricity costs across NSW, Queensland, Victoria and South Australia over 2029–30 to 2034–35, with about A$37 billion borne by other users. This is a stress-test scenario, explicitly not a demand or electricity-bill forecast. The council advocates matching new renewables and storage and making data centres bear the cost of any supply lag.
