Policy & Regulation

Proposed data-centre firming obligation could include demand curtailment
Source: RenewEconomy / AEMC | Date: 2026-10-29
URL: Original source · Supporting source
Key point: RenewEconomy reported on 29 October that Chris Bowen’s proposed rule changes would require qualifying data centres to support demand with new generation, storage and dependable demand flexibility. Enforcement options include AER penalties and possible demand curtailment for insufficient firm-contract coverage. The requests were reportedly published on 28 October. Thresholds, contract requirements and enforcement design remain unsettled: this is not an operating curtailment rule. AEMC’s earlier July advice is supporting context, not a new policy announcement.

HiTHIUM outlines Australian long-duration-storage support plans
Source: HiTHIUM company release via PR Newswire APAC | Date: 2026-10-29

Companies & Projects

Australia and PLN sign electricity-transition cooperation MoU
Source: Australian Embassy, Indonesia | Date: 2026-10-29
URL: Original source
Key point: Australia’s embassy in Indonesia announced an MoU with PLN on 29 October covering technical cooperation, finance and investment facilitation, and workforce capability. It supports grid modernisation, reliability and renewable integration, with KINETIK coordinating Australian government support. This is an Australian overseas energy-transition cooperation framework, not approval of a particular power project, a storage order or a newly quantified funding commitment.
CATL signs Australian battery integration and training MoUs
Source: CATL company release via PR Newswire | Date: 2026-10-29
URL: Original source
Key point: CATL announced MoUs with Hitachi Energy, EPEC Group and UNSW Sydney on 29 October. Proposed work covers battery–power-conversion integration, grid-compliance validation and a renewable-energy training and innovation centre in Western Sydney. These are cooperation frameworks, not commissioned assets or firm project orders.

Grid & Related Infrastructure

APA clarifies early-stage status of Keralup gas-and-battery hub
Source: APA / WA EPA | Date: 2026-10-29
URL: Original source · Supporting source
Key point: APA said on 29 October that Keralup remains in early planning, with an environmental referral lodged but no customer offtake agreements executed. The proposal combines gas generation and grid-scale batteries, with possible later renewables. EPA documents describe temporary 210 MW and permanent 660 MW gas facilities; battery capacity is unspecified. This is a developer clarification, not approval or construction.
