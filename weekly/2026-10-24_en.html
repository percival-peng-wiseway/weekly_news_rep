Solar, Storage & Markets

Quarterly analysis: batteries reduce solar offloading, but network curtailment rises
Source: RenewEconomy / AEMO | Date: 2026-10-24
URL: Original source · Quarterly report
Key point: A 24 October analysis examines curtailment data not detailed in the previous briefing. AEMO’s Q3 report records grid-scale solar economic offloading down 54% year-on-year to an average 149 MW, or 5.9% of available output, linked to battery charging and daytime demand. Meanwhile, network- or security-constrained wind and solar curtailment reached a Q3 high of 281 MW. These are different constraints: reduced economic offloading does not mean network bottlenecks have disappeared.

Wind & Project Developments

All 18 turbine foundations completed at WA’s 108 MW Waddi wind farm
Source: RenewEconomy / Tilt Renewables | Date: 2026-10-24
URL: Original source · Project page
Key point: Reporting on 24 October confirms all 18 concrete turbine foundations at Tilt Renewables’ 108 MW Waddi wind farm in WA were completed that week. Construction began in August. The next stage is preparation for turbine tower deliveries, expected to start in early 2027. The developer still lists the project as under construction; foundation completion is not turbine installation, grid connection or commercial operation.
