Source: Clean Energy Council | Date: 2026-09-29
URL: Original article
Key point: The Clean Energy Council’s January–June 2026 report records 1.89 GW of new rooftop solar across more than 180,000 installations. Battery Storage (BESS)

TagEnergy’s proposed 1 GW / 4 GWh Morwell battery enters federal assessment
Source: RenewEconomy | Date: 2026-09-29
URL: Original article
Key point: RenewEconomy reported that TagEnergy submitted the proposed Morwell battery at Hazelwood North, Victoria, for federal environmental assessment. Designed for up to 1,000 MW / 4,000 MWh, it received state development approval in June, but a grid connection agreement and final investment decision remained outstanding; the referral is not final approval or construction commencement.

Company & Project Developments

GEM analysis highlights contracting bottleneck for approved wind and solar projects
Source: RenewEconomy / Green Energy Markets | Date: 2026-09-29
URL: Original article
Key point: In analysis published on 29 September, Green Energy Markets’ Tristan Edis cited the GEM–IEEFA scorecard: more than 46 GW of wind and solar projects held planning and environmental approvals at 30 June, while 4,716 MW was under construction. He identified limited long-term offtake contracting as a key constraint; the figures describe the existing pipeline, not new approvals or construction on the publication date.
