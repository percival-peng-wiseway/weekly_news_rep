Solar

CEC records 1.89 GW of rooftop solar and over 276,000 home batteries in H1 2026
Source: Clean Energy Council | Date: 2026-09-29
URL: Original article
Key point: The Clean Energy Council’s January–June 2026 report records 1.89 GW of new rooftop solar across more than 180,000 installations. Home-battery installations reached 276,011, up 52% on the preceding six months; these figures cover completed installations in the first half of 2026, rather than September additions.
Mutitjulu utilities upgrade includes 1 MW solar and battery storage
Source: Australian Government | Date: 2026-09-29
URL: Original article
Key point: The federal government announced an A$122 million power, water and sewerage upgrade for Mutitjulu and neighbouring communities in the Northern Territory, including a 1 MW solar array and battery storage. Exact Contracting has the contract; preparation is expected in October 2026, full construction in January subject to weather, and completion in late 2028.

Battery Storage (BESS)

TagEnergy’s proposed 1 GW / 4 GWh Morwell battery enters federal assessment
Source: RenewEconomy | Date: 2026-09-29
URL: Original article
Key point: RenewEconomy reported that TagEnergy submitted the proposed Morwell battery at Hazelwood North, Victoria, for federal environmental assessment. Designed for up to 1,000 MW / 4,000 MWh, it received state development approval in June, but a grid connection agreement and final investment decision remained outstanding; the referral is not final approval or construction commencement.

Company & Project Developments

GEM analysis highlights contracting bottleneck for approved wind and solar projects
Source: RenewEconomy / Green Energy Markets | Date: 2026-09-29
URL: Original article
Key point: In analysis published on 29 September, Green Energy Markets’ Tristan Edis cited the GEM–IEEFA scorecard: more than 46 GW of wind and solar projects held planning and environmental approvals at 30 June, while 4,716 MW was under construction. He identified limited long-term offtake contracting as a key constraint; the figures describe the existing pipeline, not new approvals or construction on the publication date.
EORA and Tulla form joint venture for integrated energy projects
Source: Energy-Storage.News / EORA Energy | Date: 2026-09-29
URL: Original article
Key point: EORA Energy and Tulla Group formed a joint venture to develop integrated energy infrastructure for mining, data centres and other power-intensive sites. Its first reference project is planned at Tulla’s Norseman accommodation operation in Western Australia, combining solar, batteries, existing diesel generation and grid supply. The partnership is a development platform; no completed deployment is established.

Infrastructure & Transmission

Major earthworks progress at Copperstring’s Flinders Substation
Source: Queensland Government | Date: 2026-09-29
URL: Original article
Key point: The Queensland Government confirmed on 29 September that major earthworks, site preparation, a new access road and entrance upgrades are under way at the new Flinders Substation south of Hughenden. Bulk earthworks are expected to finish by the end of 2026; this is a construction milestone for Copperstring, not commissioning of the entire transmission line.
Two Victorian councils leave VNI West technical reference group
Source: Northern Grampians Shire Council | Date: 2026-09-29
URL: Original article
Key point: Northern Grampians and Buloke shire councils announced their withdrawal from the VNI West environmental-assessment technical reference group, citing concerns that their feedback had little influence and review work strained resources. They said they would continue engaging with the proponent and representing local interests through the EES inquiry process. The withdrawal does not cancel the transmission project.
