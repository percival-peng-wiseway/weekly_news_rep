Policy & regulation

Sundown solar and battery project secures conditional federal approval
RenewEconomy / NSW Independent Planning Commission · 2026-11-03
Original report · NSW planning decision
RenewEconomy reported on 3 November that Recurrent Energy’s Sundown solar-and-battery project in NSW’s New England REZ had secured EPBC approval, signed on 23 October following state approval in August. Conditions include biodiversity offsets and an approved, publicly available management plan. The project includes a 150 MW, four-hour battery. This is an approvals milestone, not a construction start; remaining grid-connection and other requirements were not confirmed in the report.

Solar

Gokin introduces back-contact modules for early-2027 Australian supply
pv magazine Australia · 2026-11-03
Original report
Gokin’s 495–510 W back-contact modules target Australian residential and commercial rooftops. The 3 November report says the series is CEC certified, with Australian availability expected in early 2027. Efficiency of up to 25% is a product specification, not a guaranteed yield on every roof. Certification should be distinguished from the expected supply date.

Battery storage

EP Cube gains CEC approval for three-phase storage
pv magazine Australia · 2026-11-03
Original report
A 3 November report describes EP Cube’s CEC-approved 12 kW three-phase system, with 10–40 kWh per stack and up to 200 kWh across five parallel units. The supplier says this model is ready for Australian installation. It is distinct from the next-generation single-phase product expected in Q2 2027; site and network requirements still apply.
Waroona Stage One signs A$280 million debt facility
PV Tech · 2026-11-03
Original report
Coverage on 3 November follows Frontier’s 2 November disclosure of a debt facility of up to A$280 million for Waroona Stage One: 132 MW solar and an 81.5 MW/562 MWh battery. Financial close, expected in November, remains subject to conditions including the CIS agreement and interest-rate hedging. First drawdown is forecast for Q1 2027; signing is not drawdown or commissioning.

Wind

Dinner Hill cuts turbine numbers and proposes longer-duration storage
RenewEconomy / OX2 · 2026-11-03
Original report · OX2 project and consultation details
RenewEconomy’s 3 November report details OX2’s revised Dinner Hill proposal in WA: up to 131 turbines and 943 MW, down from 170 turbines and 1.2 GW. The ancillary battery remains up to 300 MW, with four-to-eight-hour duration. OX2 confirms these specifications and distinguishes it from the separately developed Harvest BESS. Development-application comments close at 4 pm WST on 20 November. Planning, environmental and grid-connection approvals remain necessary.
Moreton Hill receives Victorian planning approval
Renewables Now / Victorian planning register · 2026-11-03
Original report · Victorian permit register
Renewables Now reported on 3 November that Squadron Energy’s proposed 420 MW Moreton Hill Wind Farm had received Victorian planning approval. The state register records a permit decision dated 30 October for application PA2604445, covering a wind energy facility, powerline, vegetation removal and associated works. The verified milestone is state planning permission. It does not establish that every environmental approval, a final investment decision or construction has been completed.

Companies & projects

FTC explores Australian steel supply with four local partners
pv magazine Australia · 2026-11-03
Original report
FTC Solar has signed a non-binding memorandum with Surdex Steel, GB Galvanizing, Australian Rollforming and Roman Steel, according to coverage dated 3 November. It explores locally fabricated steel and structural components for utility-scale solar. Future supply requires separate agreements and project-specific technical and commercial approval; the framework is not a firm order or demonstrated cost saving.
Australian Infradebt backs Lightyears’ New Zealand pipeline
Lightyears / pv magazine Australia · 2026-11-03
Original report · Company release · 2026-11-03
On 3 November, Lightyears announced NZ$174 million comprising a NZ$100 million facility from Australian lender Infradebt and NZ$74 million of planned Obayashi investment. It supports more than 300 MW of New Zealand solar and storage development over five years, with equity deployed against milestones. Included for its Australian financing link, this is not new Australian generating capacity or a completed pipeline.

Grid & related infrastructure

Meridien energy and data-centre consultation closes on 4 November
National Indigenous Times · 2026-11-03
Original report
National Indigenous Times reported on 3 November that public submissions on Project Meridien, on Karajarri Country, close on 4 November. This updates consultation on an existing proposal, not a new approval. Plans include a 1 GW data centre, 5.2 GW of solar, 1.5 GW of wind, gas generation and storage. Environmental groups question clearing and ecological impacts; proponents emphasise Traditional Owner participation while gas supply and transport arrangements remain unresolved.
