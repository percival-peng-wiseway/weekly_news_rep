Battery Storage (BESS)

Average home-battery size halves after rebate changes while installations continue to grow
RenewEconomy · 2026-08-06 · Source
Key point: By the end of July 2026, more than 490,000 Australian home batteries had been installed through the rebate, adding over 13 GWh of behind-the-meter storage; July registered volume was 960 MWh. Since the May scheme adjustment, average system size has halved from its peak and the 20–30 kWh band has become the largest by capacity.
Australian Vanadium and Alcoa assess 50–80 MW vanadium-flow battery in Western Australia
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-08-06 · Source
Key point: Australian Vanadium and its subsidiary VSUN Energy signed a non-binding MoU with Alcoa of Australia to assess a 50–80 MW, six-to-eight-hour vanadium-flow battery at Western Australian alumina refineries. The proposed system would store 400–640 MWh and the MoU has an 18-month term.
Zebre refers Noblevale 180 MW/360 MWh Queensland battery project for federal environmental review
RenewEconomy · 2026-08-06 · Source
Key point: Storage developer Zebre has referred the proposed Noblevale battery at the former Swanbank coal mine and power-station site in Ipswich, Queensland, under the Environment Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act. The 180 MW/360 MWh project received local development approval last year.

RenewEconomy · 2026-08-06 · Source
Key point: RE Future has secured planning permits for three distribution-connected wind projects in central-west Victoria with combined proposed capacity of up to 130 MW: the 40 MW Brewster, 35 MW Swansons Lane and eight-turbine Mumblin projects. Horizon Power seeks approval for solar-battery hybrid to replace Halls Creek diesel and gas supply
RenewEconomy · 2026-08-06 · Source
Key point: Horizon Power is seeking federal approval for up to 4 MW of solar, a 2 MW/2 MWh battery and a new gas plant of up to 4 MW for Halls Creek in Western Australia, replacing existing diesel generators and gas generation.
