Policy & Regulation

Post-2030 contract design for new renewables takes shape as working group agrees firming price caps
RenewEconomy · 2026-08-24 · Source
Key point: A working group has progressed post-2030 contract design for new renewables and agreed price caps for firming. The report focuses on developing contractual arrangements for new renewable capacity.
AEMO says new renewables and batteries improve reliability outlook, but projects must be built on time
RenewEconomy · 2026-08-24 · Source
Key point: The Australian Energy Market Operator said new renewable generation and batteries have improved the grid reliability outlook. It also said more projects need to be built on time.
Federal government opens WA CIS Tender 11 for 1.8 GW of wind and solar
RenewEconomy · 2026-08-25 · Source
Key point: Federal Energy Minister Chris Bowen opened Capacity Investment Scheme Tender 11 in Western Australia, seeking 1.8 GW of new wind and solar generation. It is the first tender to require proponents to have commenced a Developer Rating Scheme assessment; WA’s last state-owned coal generators are due to retire by the end of 2029.
Victoria opens Australia’s first offshore wind auction, targeting 2 GW
RenewEconomy · 2026-08-25 · Source
Key point: Victoria has opened the Request for Proposal process for its first 2 GW of offshore wind projects, Australia’s first offshore wind capacity auction. The process runs until August 2027, contracts are expected to be awarded in 2028, and the state aims to have this capacity in place by 2032.

Solar

Mass-scale reuse pilot finds 15% of second-life solar modules fail safety requirements
pv magazine Australia · 2026-08-24 · Source
Key point: A mass-scale reuse pilot found that 15% of second-life solar modules did not meet safety requirements. The result indicates that safety screening remains necessary for reuse of retired modules.
Gentari achieves mechanical completion at NSW Maryvale DC-coupled solar and storage project
pv magazine Australia · 2026-08-25 · Source
Key point: Gentari Australia announced mechanical completion of the Maryvale project near Dubbo in central-western New South Wales, comprising 243 MWp of solar PV and a 172 MW/409 MWh battery energy storage system. Physical construction has finished, with commissioning and grid connection next; earlier estimates indicated commercial operations in Q2 2027.
Solmech completes 1.2 MWh DC-coupled storage retrofit at SA’s Warnertown Solar Farm
pv magazine Australia · 2026-08-26 · Source
Key point: Solmech completed a hybrid retrofit at the 4.1 MW Warnertown Solar Farm, about 215 km north of Adelaide, adding 1.2 MWh of storage. The DC-coupled configuration is intended to avoid transformer modifications and improve operating flexibility in South Australia’s wholesale market.

Battery Storage (BESS)

Quinbrook selects GE Vernova and CATL for 1,216 MWh Supernode Stage 3 in Queensland
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-08-24 · Source
Key point: Quinbrook selected GE Vernova and CATL for Stage 3 of its Supernode battery storage project in Queensland. The 1,216 MWh stage will expand Australia’s largest battery storage system.
Battery storage investors face pressure as NEM arbitrage spreads narrow
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-08-25 · Source
Key point: At the Battery Asset Management Summit Australia 2026 in Sydney, Fluence energy markets director Matt Grover said narrower arbitrage spreads and lower revenues in the National Electricity Market are pressuring emerging BESS business cases. He linked the condition to delayed coal-retirement expectations and described it as a short-term market cycle.
DC-coupled solar and storage projects can cut Australian grid-connection approvals by up to six months
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-08-26 · Source
Key point: Sungrow Australia’s Alistair Gibson said DC-coupled projects are assessed as one integrated solar-and-battery system and can reduce the Generator Performance Standards or connection process by up to six months compared with AC-coupled designs. He said the architecture can also lower capital expenditure by requiring fewer inverters.
HMC says equity is committed for its first large battery, with about 2 GW moving toward FID
RenewEconomy · 2026-08-26 · Source
Key point: HMC Capital says its Illuma Energy platform has committed equity for its first battery project and has about 2 GW of near-term renewable and storage developments moving toward final investment decision over the next few years. The first project is expected to be the 300 MW/1,200 MWh, approximately A$800 million Moorabool BESS in Victoria.

Wind

Concrete pours begin for 38 turbine foundations at Queensland’s Boulder Creek wind project
RenewEconomy · 2026-08-26 · Source
Key point: Aula Energy has begun turbine-foundation pours at the 228 MW Boulder Creek wind project, 40 km south-west of Rockhampton in Central Queensland, alongside trenching, cabling, switching-station and road works. The 38-turbine project is expected to power more than 85,000 homes when fully operational and avoid about 379,000 tonnes of CO2 emissions annually.

Company & Project Developments

Allegro strikes deal to commercialise flow battery technology
pv magazine Australia · 2026-08-24 · Source
Key point: Allegro has struck an agreement to commercialise its flow battery technology. The transaction marks progress towards commercialisation of Australian flow battery technology.
Pacific Energy signs 10-year PPA for hybrid solar and battery power station at WA’s Youanmi gold project
pv magazine Australia · 2026-08-25 · Source
Key point: Pacific Energy signed a 10-year power purchase agreement with Rox Resources to design, build, own and operate an off-grid power station for the Youanmi gold project in Western Australia’s remote Mid West. It will include 25 MW of solar, 9.3 MVA of battery storage, 25 MW of gas generation and up to 2 MW of backup diesel; Rox is targeting first gold in mid-2027.

Infrastructure & Transmission

TasNetworks plans to proceed after winning North West Transmission Developments planning appeal
RenewEconomy · 2026-08-25 · Source
Key point: After the Tasmanian Civil and Administrative Tribunal dismissed a planning appeal, TasNetworks said it would soon issue a notice to proceed to head contractor Genus. Stage one includes up to 130 km of transmission lines and substation upgrades, providing connection for the Marinus Link subsea cable.
