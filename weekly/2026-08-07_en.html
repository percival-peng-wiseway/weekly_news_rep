Policy & Regulation

Federal Safeguard Mechanism review opens on alignment with 2030 and 2035 targets
RenewEconomy · 2026-08-07 · Source
Key point: Energy Minister Chris Bowen launched a review of the Safeguard Mechanism to assess whether its current settings can support Australia’s 2030 climate target and how it should change for the following decade. The mechanism covers more than 200 facilities emitting over 100,000 tonnes of greenhouse gases annually; consultation closes on 18 September and a report is due in early 2027.

Solar

Anna Creek Station in South Australia deploys 49 kW solar-and-battery microgrid
pv magazine Australia · 2026-08-07 · Source
Key point: Anna Creek Station in South Australia, owned by Williams Cattle Company, has deployed 49 kW of Hanwha Q Cells rooftop solar with Pylontech lithium batteries, generator backup and remote monitoring as its primary power supply. It is one of seven remote-area power systems delivered by MyEnergy for the company in South Australia under a program worth more than AUD 1.5 million.

Battery Storage (BESS)

ZEBRE refers Queensland’s 180 MW/360 MWh Noblevale Battery for federal environmental assessment
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-08-07 · Source
Key point: ZEBRE, a joint venture between HD Renewable Energy and ZEN Energy, has referred the Noblevale Battery to the EPBC Act federal assessment process. The proposed 180 MW/360 MWh, two-hour system would be built on a repurposed mining site near Swanbank in Ipswich, Queensland, about 45 km south-west of Brisbane.
July home-battery registered volume reaches about 990 MWh, around triple year-on-year
pv magazine Australia · 2026-08-07 · Source
Key point: SunWiz data puts Australian home-battery registered volume at about 990 MWh in July 2026, down 1.55% from June but around three times the 320 MWh recorded in July 2025. The national average system size fell to 22.6 kWh, its third consecutive monthly decline.

Wind

Victoria confirms Australia’s first offshore wind auction remains scheduled for this month
RenewEconomy · 2026-08-07 · Source
Key point: Victoria’s new energy minister, Lily Symes, said the state’s first offshore wind auction—and Australia’s first—will proceed as planned in August, although no firm date has been published. The statement responds to market speculation that a state leadership change could delay the process.
Tasmania’s Whaleback Ridge wind project seeks federal approval for up to 2.8 GW
RenewEconomy · 2026-08-07 · Source
Key point: Local developer Westcoast Renewable Energy has referred the Whaleback Ridge project in Tasmania for EPBC assessment as a phased development of up to 2.8 GW, with up to 280 turbines rated at up to 10 MW each. The referral also includes up to nine collector substations, four switching substations and underground and overhead connections to an existing 220 kV line.
