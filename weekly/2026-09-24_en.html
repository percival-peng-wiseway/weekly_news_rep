SeaTwirl and HFLOW propose 20 MW floating-wind demonstration off Newcastle
RenewEconomy · 2026-09-24 · Source · SeaTwirl investor release (2026-09-22)
Key point: RenewEconomy reported on a five-year memorandum of understanding signed on 22 September by SeaTwirl and Hunter Floating Offshore Wind (HFLOW) for a proposed 20 MW demonstrator using 2 MW vertical-axis turbines. HFLOW is applying for a research and demonstration licence; the MoU contains no delivery or investment commitment and does not oblige either party to proceed.

ESR agrees to acquire Aquila Clean Energy APAC, including its Australian development platform
ESR · 2026-09-24 · Source · RenewEconomy (2026-09-24)
Key point: ESR signed an agreement to buy 100% of Aquila Clean Energy APAC from Aquila Group, with completion expected by Q1 2027 subject to approvals and closing conditions. Its 1.6 GW portfolio spans operating, construction and advanced-development assets across several Asia-Pacific markets, including Australia; RenewEconomy identifies five Australian projects in development, comprising Port Latta wind and four South Australian batteries.
