Policy & Regulation

Victoria outlines transition of household solar support to VEU from July 2027
Source: Solar Victoria | Date: 2026-10-21
URL: Original source
Key point: Solar Victoria published an update on 21 October explaining the government’s 17 October announcement that household solar support will move into Victorian Energy Upgrades from 1 July 2027. Current PV rebates and optional interest-free loans remain available until 30 June 2027. The new residential PV activity is still being designed; future incentive levels and implementation details have not been confirmed.

Adaminaby 4.99 MW solar-and-battery proposal faces community objections
Source: RenewEconomy | Date: 2026-10-21
URL: Original source
Key point: RenewEconomy reported on 21 October that EDPR Australia’s proposed 4.99 MW solar-and-battery project near Old Adaminaby, NSW, faced local concerns over visual impacts, glare and traffic at a recent hearing. Community fatigue over Snowy 2.0 construction formed part of the context. The article covers the previous week’s hearing and planning dispute, not a same-day approval. The project remains a proposal, not a construction milestone.

Battery Storage

Ten AusNet street batteries operating to support rooftop solar
Source: AusNet | Date: 2026-10-21
URL: Original source
Key point: AusNet announced on 21 October that ten pole-mounted neighbourhood batteries were operational across Victorian communities serving 1,777 households. Delivered through the state’s 100 Neighbourhood Battery Program, they store surplus daytime rooftop solar for later use and support voltage management. Aggregate MW/MWh capacity was not disclosed. The stated supply capability under normal grid conditions should not be read as a blackout-backup guarantee.
Fox ESS launches Power Cube all-in-one home storage in Australia
Source: SolarQuotes | Date: 2026-10-21
URL: Original source
Key point: Reporting on 21 October confirmed the Australian launch of Fox ESS’s Power Cube, integrating an inverter with two to nine 5.46 kWh battery modules for 10.92–49.14 kWh of storage. According to the manufacturer, 5–12 kW single-phase models are CEC-approved, while 5–15 kW three-phase models are still forthcoming. These are launch specifications, not independent performance results. Backup output remains constrained by inverter ratings and local connection requirements.

Company & Project Developments

ARENA stages funding for Yarwun’s 360 MWh thermal-storage demonstration
Source: ARENA | Date: 2026-10-21
URL: Original source
Key point: ARENA announced staged support on 21 October for Rondo thermal storage at Rio Tinto’s Yarwun alumina refinery in Queensland: A$3.6 million for front-end engineering and design, up to A$38 million for deployment after a successful study, and up to A$33.3 million in conditional contingency support. The proposed system has 30 MW thermal discharge, 78 MW electrical charging and 360 MWh storage. It is an industrial-heat demonstration, not an operating electrochemical BESS or an unconditional grant of the full funding envelope.
GreenPoint offers up to 75 Koolunga battery roles to affected Whyalla workers
Source: Utility Magazine | Date: 2026-10-21
URL: Original source
Key point: Utility Magazine reported on 21 October that GreenPoint Energy would offer up to 75 roles at its Koolunga battery project to workers affected by the Whyalla Steelworks blast-furnace closure. The opportunities target electrical, technical-supervision, high-voltage industrial and construction skills. The project reached financial close in June 2026. The new development is a workforce-transition offer, not confirmation that 75 people have been hired or that the battery is operating.

Infrastructure & Grid

ARENA backs up to 130 ACT fleet EVs with A$4 million for V2G
Source: ARENA | Date: 2026-10-21
URL: Original source
Key point: ARENA announced A$4 million for JET Charge on 21 October to deploy up to 130 EVs and bidirectional chargers across the ACT Government fleet through REVS2. The project will test incentives and commercial viability for grid services alongside everyday transport duties. This is funding for planned deployment, not confirmation that all 130 vehicles are connected or that commercial returns have been proven.
