AEMO forecasts low-demand risks in South Australia for 7–8 November
AEMO · 2026-11-01
AEMO 145956 · AEMO 145964 · AEMO 145965 · AEMO 145966
AEMO published four notices on 1 November seeking a market response to forecast minimum-system-load risks in South Australia. For 7 November, notice 145956 forecasts MSL1 conditions from 11:30 to 15:30 AEST, with minimum operational demand of −141 MW at 14:30. For 8 November, notice 145964 forecasts MSL2 conditions from 12:00 to 12:30 AEST, with a minimum of −336 MW at 12:30; notices 145965 and 145966 identify adjoining MSL1 periods. These are forecasts, not confirmed events or notices of outages or solar curtailment. AEMO had not yet estimated the latest time intervention might be required. The operational-demand measure excludes significant non-scheduled generation and is not total household consumption. All quoted times are NEM market time (AEST): add one hour for Melbourne (AEDT), or 30 minutes for South Australia (ACDT). This is an operational outlook, not a new project commissioning or a new regulation.
