Solar

Ingeteam commissions 236MVA of solar capacity in NSW and Queensland
pv magazine Australia · 2026-09-04 · Source
Key point: Ingeteam supplied 144 inverters for Metlen’s six utility-scale solar projects in New South Wales and Queensland, which are fully commissioned with a combined capacity of 236MVA. The portfolio includes Corowa, Junee, Wagga Wagga and Wyalong in NSW’s Riverina region, and Kingaroy in Queensland.
Victoria’s SEC connects 119MW solar component at Horsham to the grid
PV Tech · 2026-09-04 · Source
Key point: Victoria’s State Electricity Commission (SEC) connected the 119MW solar component of the Horsham Renewable Energy Park to the grid for the first time, after all 212,296 modules were installed in late 2025. Its co-located 100MW/200MWh battery has not yet arrived on site, and the project is planned to enter operation in 2027.
ARENA awards AU$4.1 million for solar-farm operations platform
pv magazine Australia · 2026-09-04 · Source
Key point: The Australian Renewable Energy Agency (ARENA) has awarded AU$4.1 million to Melbourne-based Proa Energy’s HiveOps platform within a AU$10.5 million project to identify solar-farm faults faster and optimise maintenance decisions. Proa will deliver the project with Potentia Energy and Equans Solar & Storage, with completion scheduled for 2028.

Battery Storage (BESS)

Energy Vault acquires site for NSW’s 1GWh Stoney Creek battery project
RenewEconomy · 2026-09-04 · Source
Key point: Following Foreign Investment Review Board approval, Energy Vault completed the acquisition of the Stoney Creek BESS project site near Narrabri, New South Wales. The 125MW, up-to-1,000MWh project has a 14-year NSW Long-Term Energy Service Agreement and is expected to begin construction in early 2027, targeting commercial operation in the first half of 2028, subject to final approvals.

Wind

First turbine completed at Queensland’s 285MW Lotus Creek wind farm
RenewEconomy · 2026-09-04 · Source
Key point: The first “golden turbine” has been completed at the 285MW Lotus Creek wind farm in central Queensland, which is planned to have 46 turbines. State utility CS Energy owns the project outright after acquiring it from Copenhagen Infrastructure Partners in August 2024 for AU$1.3 billion.

Company & Project Developments

1414 Degrees takes full ownership of South Australia’s Aurora Energy Precinct
RenewEconomy · 2026-09-04 · Source
Key point: 1414 Degrees acquired Vast Solar Aurora for AU$350,000, securing full ownership of the Aurora Energy Precinct near Port Augusta, South Australia, and its associated development approvals. Approved configurations include up to a 140MW/280MWh battery, 70MW of solar PV and 150MW of CSP; the company has also signed a heads of agreement for up to 1GW of AI data-centre infrastructure.
