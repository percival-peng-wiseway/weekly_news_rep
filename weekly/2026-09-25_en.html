Australian Energy Statistics 2026 released with data through 2024–25

Key point: The federal department published the Australian Energy Update 2026 and its accompanying statistics on 25 September. Solar

Ausgrid trial targets 70 MW of rooftop solar and 130 MWh of shared batteries
RenewEconomy · 2026-09-25 · Source · Ausgrid (2026-09-18)
Key point: A 25 September report details Ausgrid’s Community Power Network trial in Botany–Mascot and Charmhaven, intended to support up to 70 MW of additional rooftop solar and 130 MWh of batteries. Around 32,000 customers are expected to share benefits; Ausgrid says it will invest about AUD 120 million and shareholders will carry the trial’s financial risk, with AUD 13.2 million of ARENA support.

Wind

Bungaban’s 1,400 MW wind farm and 500 MW solar project secure federal environmental approval
Minister for the Environment and Water · 2026-09-25 · Source
Key point: The federal government announced environmental approval for Windlab and Squadron Energy’s Bungaban solar and wind projects in Queensland’s Western Downs. The 500 MW solar project was assessed in 20 business days on previously cleared, degraded land; the 1,400 MW wind farm is subject to environmental conditions. These are approval milestones, not operating capacity.

Infrastructure & Transmission

Marinus Link receives new licence as early works continue for 750 MW first stage
Assistant Minister for Climate Change and Energy · 2026-09-25 · Source
Key point: The federal government announced a new licence for Marinus Link between Tasmania and Victoria. Stage 1 is planned as a 750 MW, 345 km subsea and underground interconnector, scheduled for completion in late 2030; early works were continuing ahead of major construction. This licence follows the project’s separate environmental approval in 2025.
