Policy & Regulation

PM Albanese proposes legislation: large data centres must become "net generators"
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-07-19 · Source
Key point: PM Albanese announced at the University of Sydney on 15 July that next-generation large data centres will be legally required to build their own new renewable generation and storage, inject no less power into the grid than they consume, and bear full grid connection costs. A new AI Office will be established within the Department of the Prime Minister and Cabinet, with legislation expected to be introduced early next year. Polling shows broad public support for data centres funding their own clean energy. pv magazine analysis notes the data centre boom need not spike power prices — provided stalled renewable projects are unlocked. In contrast, the Northern Territory committed land for a gas-backed data centre, drawing environmental criticism.
NSW South West REZ transmission deal sealed, unlocking 3.56 GW
pv magazine Australia · 2026-07-20 · Source
Key point: EnergyCo and Transgrid signed a project development deed to upgrade the Jerilderie–Wagga Wagga corridor (including Dinawan substation upgrade from 330kV to 500kV), unlocking 3.56 GW of solar, wind and storage projects in the South West REZ. The NSW Government had previously committed $225 million to accelerate the zone.
Queensland reportedly shelves $6 billion Mt Rawdon pumped hydro project (2 GW / 20 GWh)
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-07-14 · Source
Key point: Following a QIC review of four pumped hydro options (Mt Rawdon, Big-T, Capricornia, Borumba), the Queensland Government will reportedly not prioritise the 20 GWh Mt Rawdon gold mine conversion project. The project had previously received $50 million in government funding, with Evolution Mining holding a 50% stake.

Key point: The Clean Energy Regulator suspended 21 companies for false statements including fabricated installation volumes and forged compliance documents, signalling tougher enforcement ahead.
