Company & Project Developments

Whyalla West selection advances almost 13 GW of early-stage renewable proposals
Source: RenewEconomy / Glam Adelaide | Date: 2026-10-18
URL：Original article · Additional reporting
Key point: Reports published on 18 October identify four selected proponents for almost 13 GW of wind and solar on pastoral land west of Whyalla: Windlab (6 GW wind/solar), Altra Energy SA (4.7 GW wind/solar), Heron Energy (1.6 GW wind) and Neoen (solar). The companies have exclusive rights to apply for Renewable Feasibility Licences; this is not construction approval, financial close or operating capacity. Further studies, consultation and regulatory approvals are required; Windlab’s project website separately confirms early engagement and a multi-year development process.
