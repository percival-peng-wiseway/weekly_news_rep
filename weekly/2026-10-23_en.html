Policy & Market Reform

AEMO opens consultation on Stage 1 price-responsive resource trial
Source: AEMO | Date: 2026-10-23
URL: Original source
Key point: AEMO published an approach paper on 23 October for Stage 1 of its Integrating Price-Responsive Resources trial. Submissions on participation, operating requirements, settlement and compensation close on 27 November. The voluntary framework aims to bring currently unscheduled price-responsive resources into NEM scheduling and dispatch. This is consultation on trial arrangements, not full implementation.

Solar & Project Case Studies

MSAC case study details 1.3 MW solar array on a constrained roof
Source: AIKO Australia / pv magazine Australia | Date: 2026-10-23
URL: Original source
Key point: AIKO’s 23 October case study describes a completed 1.3 MW rooftop array at Melbourne Sports and Aquatic Centre, exceeding the original 850 kW tender. Next Green Group used 2,016 modules and flush mounting on a curved, partly shaded roof; the SEC owns and operates the system. This is a technical account of an existing installation, not a same-day commissioning announcement. Output, savings and module-performance benefits are attributed estimates or company claims, not independently verified operating results.

Battery Storage

Marysville opens 59 kW/224 kWh neighbourhood battery
Source: Victorian Government | Date: 2026-10-23
URL: Original source
Key point: The Victorian Government announced the opening of Marysville Community Centre’s neighbourhood battery and expanded rooftop solar on 23 October. The 59 kW/224 kWh battery stores renewable energy and provides backup power. Marysville is one of 11 communities in the regional resilience project. Estimated annual savings of about A$3,762 are a government projection, not realised financial results.

Wind & Grid Infrastructure

Substation energised at 414 MW Uungula wind farm
Source: Squadron Energy | Date: 2026-10-23
URL: Original source
Key point: Squadron Energy reported on 23 October that Uungula’s 330/33 kV substation had been completed and energised that week, connected through 11.6 kilometres of 330 kV transmission line. Construction of the 414 MW NSW wind farm is about two-thirds complete. Energisation enables turbine testing and commissioning, rather than signifying commercial operation of all 69 turbines. It is a new milestone beyond the previously reported registration.

Markets & Power System

AEMO quarterly data records 80.4% renewable-and-storage peak
Source: pv magazine Australia / AEMO | Date: 2026-10-23
URL: Original source · Quarterly report
Key point: AEMO data reported on 23 October shows renewables and storage supplied 80.4% of NEM generation in a half-hour on 19 September, including 51.8% distributed PV and 16.4% grid-scale solar. The quarter-wide renewable share was 44.3%, distinct from that short-period peak. Average wholesale spot prices were A$63/MWh, down 27% year-on-year; this does not imply an equivalent retail-bill reduction.
