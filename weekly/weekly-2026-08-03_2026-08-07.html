Policy & Regulation

Federal rooftop solar rebate expanded to systems up to 1 MW
pv magazine Australia · 2026-08-05 · Source
Key point: The Australian government expanded the federal rooftop solar rebate, lifting the eligible system cap from 100 kW to 1 MW. The change targets businesses, farms and community groups in the market segment between household and utility-scale systems.
Federal Safeguard Mechanism review opens consultation on fit for 2030 and 2035 targets
RenewEconomy · 2026-08-07 · Source
Key point: Energy Minister Chris Bowen launched a Safeguard Mechanism review to assess whether current settings support Australia’s 2030 climate target and how they should change over the next decade. The mechanism covers more than 200 facilities emitting over 100,000 tonnes of greenhouse gases a year; consultation closes 18 September and a report is expected in early 2027.
BNEF says Australia may miss its 82% renewables target for 2030 despite record solar and battery investment
RenewEconomy · 2026-08-04 · Source
Key point: BloombergNEF said record large-scale solar investment and household battery deployment would not offset weak wind prospects. It expects Australia to struggle to reach an 82% renewable-energy share by 2030.
EnergyAustralia admits breach over delayed Solar Sharer offer
ESD News · 2026-08-03 · Source
Key point: EnergyAustralia admitted it did not provide the Solar Sharer Offer to eligible households in New South Wales, South Australia and south-east Queensland from 1 July 2026; the offer provides three hours of free electricity daily. It went live on 3 August, and the ACCC accepted enforceable commitments requiring ongoing availability and compliance reporting.

Solar

Utility-scale solar investment reached US$3 billion in the first half of 2026
pv magazine Australia · 2026-08-06 · Source
Key point: BNEF recorded US$3 billion in Australian utility-scale solar investment in the first half of 2026, US$876 million more than in the second half of 2025. Edify Energy’s 720 MW Smoky Creek and Guthrie’s Gap projects in central Queensland were among the drivers and include a 600 MW, four-hour battery system.
Octopus Australia appoints Habitat Energy to optimise two DC-coupled solar and storage projects
pv magazine Australia · 2026-08-05 · Source
Key point: Octopus Group’s Australian business appointed Habitat Energy to provide trading and optimisation services for two early-stage DC-coupled solar and battery projects in Australia. The projects were described as among the country’s first of their kind.
July large-scale solar share set seasonal records in every state but one
RenewEconomy · 2026-08-05 · Source
Key point: Cited data showed large-scale solar set July electricity-supply-share records in every Australian state except one. Curtailment was lower than a year earlier as batteries absorbed more generation.
Anna Creek Station installs 49 kW solar-and-battery microgrid in South Australia
pv magazine Australia · 2026-08-07 · Source
Key point: Anna Creek Station installed 49 kW of rooftop PV with lithium batteries, diesel backup and remote monitoring as its primary supply. It is one of seven systems MyEnergy has delivered for the company in remote South Australia under a program valued at more than A$1.5 million.

Battery Storage (BESS)

ZEBRE submits 180 MW/360 MWh Noblevale Battery for federal environmental assessment
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-08-07 · Source
Key point: HD Renewable Energy and ZEN Energy joint venture ZEBRE submitted the Noblevale Battery for federal environmental assessment. The proposed 180 MW/360 MWh, two-hour project would be built on a repurposed mine site near Swanbank in Ipswich, Queensland.
Australian Vanadium and Alcoa assess 50–80 MW vanadium flow battery in Western Australia
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-08-06 · Source
Key point: Australian Vanadium, VSUN Energy and Alcoa of Australia signed a non-binding memorandum to assess a 50–80 MW, six-to-eight-hour vanadium flow battery at Western Australian alumina refineries. The proposed system would have 400–640 MWh of storage and the memorandum runs for 18 months.
AMPYR Australia receives AEMO 5.3.4 letter for 270 MW grid-forming battery
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-08-05 · Source
Key point: AMPYR Australia received an AEMO 5.3.4 letter for the 270 MW grid-forming Northern Battery near Port Augusta, South Australia. The letter is a key document in the project’s grid-connection process.
Household battery installations continue growing as average system size halves after rebate changes
RenewEconomy · 2026-08-06 · Source
Key point: By the end of July 2026, more than 490,000 subsidised household batteries with over 13 GWh of storage had been installed in Australia; July registrations totalled 960 MWh. Since the May rebate adjustment, average system size has halved from its peak, with 20–30 kWh the largest segment by capacity.
AEMO launches fault-current trial as grid-forming BESS capability remains unconfirmed
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-08-03 · Source
Key point: AEMO said grid-forming BESS has not yet been confirmed as able to meet the National Electricity Market’s highest minimum system-strength requirement and launched a fault-current trial. Victoria’s Koorangie BESS has deployed 100 Tesla Megapacks with grid-forming inverters.

Wind

Victoria confirms Australia’s first offshore wind auction will proceed this month
RenewEconomy · 2026-08-07 · Source
Key point: Victoria’s new Energy Minister Lily Symes said the state’s first offshore wind auction, also Australia’s first, would proceed as planned in August. A specific auction date had not been announced.
Tasmania’s Whaleback Ridge wind project seeks federal approval for up to 2.8 GW
RenewEconomy · 2026-08-07 · Source
Key point: Westcoast Renewable Energy submitted the Whaleback Ridge project in Tasmania for EPBC assessment. Its staged proposal is up to 2.8 GW and 280 turbines of up to 10 MW each, with up to nine collector substations, four switching stations and cables to an existing 220 kV line.
Three Victorian wind projects gain state permits while two await federal EPBC decisions
RenewEconomy · 2026-08-06 · Source
Key point: RE Future secured planning permits for three distribution-connected wind projects in western Victoria with combined proposed capacity of up to 130 MW, including 40 MW Brewster, 35 MW Swansons Lane and the eight-turbine Mumblin project. Two of the three still await federal EPBC decisions.
First 80-metre blades arrive at King Rocks wind farm
RenewEconomy · 2026-08-05 · Source
Key point: King Rocks wind farm safely received its first turbine blade nearly five months after its final concrete pour. The project is to receive more than 50 blades, each 80 metres long.
