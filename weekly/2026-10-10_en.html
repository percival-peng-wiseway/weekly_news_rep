Policy & Regulation

Tasmanian EPA seeks to join Firmus generator-use appeal
Source: ABC News | Date: 2026-10-10
URL：Original article
Key point: ABC reported on 10 October that Tasmania’s Environment Protection Authority applied on 7 October to join Firmus’s appeal over diesel-generator conditions for its approved 288 MW data centre; no appeal outcome was reported. Firmus’s submission says renewable capacity it commits to support may be outside Tasmania, while Hydro Tasmania says the projects it is assessing are on-island.

Battery Storage (BESS)

Revised Waratah battery agreement to allow more market participation
Source: RenewEconomy / AER / Akaysha Energy | Date: 2026-10-10
URL：Original article
Key point: RenewEconomy’s 10 October report covers revised arrangements for the operational 850 MW / 1,680 MWh Waratah battery, following AER and Akaysha announcements on 8 and 9 October. The agreement retains its 700 MW System Integrity Protection Scheme service while permitting greater market participation when that service is not needed, with revenue sharing to reduce customer costs. Akaysha expects the modified arrangements to start in late 2026.
GoodWe announces public rollout of ESA battery integration with Amber
Source: GoodWe / Amber | Date: 2026-10-10
URL：Original article
Key point: GoodWe announced on 10 October that eligible ESA home batteries can access Amber’s SmartShift optimisation in the ACT, NSW, SA, south-east Queensland and Victoria, following staged testing. Eligible existing systems do not require replacement battery hardware. Amber’s support page still lists GoodWe as open beta, so compatibility and automation depend on the system configuration.

Wind

Rehabilitation progresses at decommissioned Esperance wind farms
Source: RenewEconomy / Synergy | Date: 2026-10-10
URL：Original article
Key point: RenewEconomy reported on 10 October on Synergy’s native revegetation work with the Esperance Tjaltjraak Native Title Aboriginal Corporation at the former Ten Mile Lagoon and Nine Mile Beach wind farms. Decommissioning began in 2022, with rehabilitation work over the past year and monitoring to continue for several years. This is a site-restoration update, rather than repowering or new generation.
