ARENA marks its 100th community-battery installation and adds funding
RenewEconomy · 2026-09-09 · Source
Key point: The Australian Renewable Energy Agency (ARENA) confirmed the 100th community battery has been installed, at Horizon Power’s Regional WA Community Battery Deployment Project in Esperance. ARENA added AU$23.2 million for 47 new batteries in NSW and South Australia: Yes Group received AU$10.29 million for 14, Ausgrid AU$8 million for 21, and the City of Newcastle AU$5 million for 12.
Victorian anti-wind group files lawsuit over the 400MW Mt Fyans project
RenewEconomy · 2026-09-09 · Source
Key point: The Mortlake Community Alliance has sued Planning Minister Sonya Kilkenny and the owners of the 400MW Mt Fyans wind project in Victoria’s Supreme Court, alleging errors in the planning permit issued in 2025. The group says a successful case could lead it to pursue wider action involving public servants and historic wind permits; the planning department declined comment while the matter is before the court.

Winton North solar and storage project in Victoria installs more than 200,000 modules
pv magazine Australia · 2026-09-09 · Source
Key point: European Energy says all 200,172 PV modules are installed at its 256-hectare Winton North project near Wangaratta, Victoria. ICC Sydney to expand rooftop solar to 2MW
pv magazine Australia · 2026-09-09 · Source
Key point: The NSW government plans to replace and expand the existing 550kW rooftop system at the International Convention Centre Sydney in Darling Harbour with a 2MW array of more than 4,500 panels across over 18,400 square metres. The project is expected to generate about 2.6GWh a year and work has started.

Waratah Super Battery tests grid “shock absorber” capability at at least 700MW for two hours
RenewEconomy · 2026-09-09 · Source
Key point: NSW’s Waratah Super Battery injected at least 700MW into the grid for two hours in a test of its ability to meet the System Integrity Protection Scheme (SIPS) contract. The battery is rated at 850MW/1,680MWh and has a 5.5-year contract requiring up to 700MW of capacity at specified times.
Queensland commissions Australia’s first grid-connected industrial sodium-sulfur battery system
RenewEconomy · 2026-09-09 · Source
Key point: Queensland University of Technology (QUT), Lava Blue and partners have commissioned Australia’s first grid-connected, industrial-scale sodium-sulfur (NAS) battery at the PRiSM advanced-materials processing facility in Brisbane, to test long-duration storage integrated with on-site solar. The team next proposes a 1.5MW, at-least-six-hour sodium-sulfur battery at Swanbank near Ipswich; NGK plans to cease new NAS battery shipments after January 2027.
NEM grid-scale battery fleet earns AU$28.79 million in August, with capture rate rising to 54%
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-09-09 · Source
Key point: NEMPulse estimates that 58 grid-scale batteries in the NEM earned a combined AU$28.79 million in gross energy and FCAS revenue in August 2026, 6% more than July. Active fleet capacity reached 8,983MW/21,539MWh by month-end, while the aggregate revenue-capture rate increased from 48% in July to 54%.
