Proposed Collie steel mill signs renewable power agreement with BHP
Source: RenewEconomy / Generation Steel | Date: 2026-10-25
URL: Original source · Company release
Key point: Generation Steel announced a binding PPA with BHP on 25 October for its proposed Collie mill, drawing on operating wind and solar generation. RenewEconomy identifies Flat Rocks wind and Merredin solar, with contracts expected to start in 2029. The planned A$850 million electric-arc furnace would recycle scrap, targeting 450,000 tonnes a year and annual electricity use of about 250,000 MWh. This is a power-contract milestone, not evidence of commissioning or a final investment decision. The company’s all-renewable electricity ambition does not establish hourly carbon-free supply or zero lifecycle emissions.
