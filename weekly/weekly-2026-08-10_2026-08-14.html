Policy & Regulation

Federal government launches Safeguard Mechanism review on limiting offsets
ESD News · 2026-08-10 · Source
Key point: The Albanese government has launched a Safeguard Mechanism review that will examine caps on emissions offsets, domestic abatement, industrial decarbonisation, and energy security through electrification and Australian renewables. Submissions close 18 September 2026 and the review is due in early 2027.
Australia advances renewable-energy and storage compliance framework for large data centres
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-08-11 · Source
Key point: The Australian Energy Market Commission proposed four measures requiring large data centres to match demand with new renewable certificates, provide peak capacity and register qualifying facilities as NEM participants. NSW is considering a Renewable Energy Zone-style connection regime for loads of 5 MW and above; federal legislation is expected in early 2027.
CEFC commits record AU$9.1 billion in FY26, mobilising AU$19.6 billion in project value
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-08-11 · Source
Key point: CEFC committed AU$9.1 billion across 45 new transactions in FY26 to 30 June 2026, mobilising AU$19.6 billion of transaction value. Its Rewiring the Nation fund committed AU$7.2 billion, including AU$3.8 billion for the 750 MW first stage of Marinus Link.
Australia’s largest aluminium smelter receives AU$2.5 billion support for coal-to-renewables shift
RenewEconomy · 2026-08-12 · Source
Key point: A AU$2.5 billion agreement will support the Tomago smelter’s shift from coal power to wind and solar. The proposal would have the smelter act like a large battery to support the energy transition.

Solar

Aldi expands rooftop solar and battery packages to Perth and Adelaide
pv magazine Australia · 2026-08-10 · Source
Key point: Aldi and Tempo Group have expanded packages to selected Perth and Adelaide suburbs, offering 7 kW or 7.9 kW PV, a 6 kW hybrid inverter and 5–20 kWh batteries. A 7 kW system with a 5 kWh LFP battery starts at AU$7,499.
Engie commissions Victoria’s largest fully operational solar farm
pv magazine Australia · 2026-08-10 · Source
Key point: Engie commissioned the 250 MW Goorambat East Solar Farm near Benalla in north-east Victoria, with about 500,000 modules across 630 hectares. It is Engie’s first large-scale Australian solar project and Victoria’s largest fully operational solar facility.
ClearVue’s 600 W rooftop PV panel gains Clean Energy Council approval
pv magazine Australia · 2026-08-11 · Source
Key point: Perth-based ClearVue Technologies received CEC approval for its ClearVue-Helios rooftop module, making it grid-connectable and subsidy-eligible. The module is rated up to 600 W at 220 W/m² and carries 30-year performance and 15-year product warranties.
Edify Energy reaches financial close on two Queensland solar-plus-storage projects
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-08-14 · Source
Key point: Edify reached financial close on the Ganymirra and Majors Creek projects near Townsville: 360 MWp of solar and 300 MW/1,200 MWh of BESS in total. The Woodstock projects will use reverse DC coupling and a grid-forming inverter hybrid architecture.

Battery Storage (BESS)

Homebush 200 MW/431 MWh battery approved in Sydney
RenewEconomy · 2026-08-10 · Source
Key point: The NSW Independent Planning Commission approved Ausgrid’s 200 MW/431 MWh battery beside Homebush substation, a AU$215 million project about 13 km from the Sydney CBD. Ausgrid hopes to start construction this year.
Zenith seeks retail licence for wind, solar and storage supply to Kalgoorlie Super Pit
RenewEconomy · 2026-08-11 · Source
Key point: Zenith Energy proposes 256 MW of wind, 110 MWac of solar and a 138 MW/300 MWh battery for the Eastern Goldfields Power Project, targeting about 70% renewable supply to the Kalgoorlie Super Pit mine.
Australian batteries set 4,325 MW NEM discharge record
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-08-12 · Source
Key point: At 18:25 on 11 August, NEM battery discharge reached 4,325 MW, above the 4,131 MW record set on 31 July, supplying 14.42% of NEM demand. Batteries moved from nearly 4 GW charging to more than 4.3 GW discharging, an approximately 8.3 GW intraday swing.
Octopus progresses 64 MW/128 MWh DC-coupled BESS at Fulham
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-08-12 · Source
Key point: After installing Fulham’s 80 MW solar array, Octopus Australia is progressing a 64 MW/128 MWh DC-coupled battery supplied by Wärtsilä. Delivery is expected in early H2 2026 and operation is planned for 2027.
EPEC wins high-voltage grid-connection contract for Lower Wonga solar-plus-storage project
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-08-12 · Source
Key point: EPEC will build, test and commission a 275/33 kV substation and 275 kV overhead line for the Lower Wonga Solar Farm near Gympie. The project includes 380 MWdc solar and 281 MW/843 MWh of battery storage.
BW ESS acquires 250 MW/1,000 MWh Yanco battery project in NSW Riverina
pv magazine Australia · 2026-08-13 · Source
Key point: BW ESS acquired the 250 MW/1,000 MWh Yanco BESS in the NSW Riverina from Australian developer ACEnergy.
Hithium to supply 421 MWh BESS for Queensland’s Fraser Coast project
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-08-13 · Source
Key point: Hithium signed a supply agreement with GPG for a 421 MWh grid-forming BESS at Fraser Coast. The Queensland project is planned for commercial operation in 2028 and will connect via a 275 kV line to Powerlink’s Teebar substation.
Origin’s 300 MW/650 MWh Mortlake battery begins commercial operation
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-08-14 · Source
Key point: Origin confirmed commercial operation following major commissioning of the 300 MW/650 MWh Mortlake BESS in south-west Victoria. The AU$400 million project connects to AusNet’s 500 kV switching station.

AGL says coal plants will close on schedule but seeks certainty for wind investment
RenewEconomy · 2026-08-12 · Source
Key point: AGL says it will meet its announced coal-plant closure dates, but wants policy and regulatory certainty before building more wind. It also calls for stronger obligations in Capacity Investment Scheme contracts.
