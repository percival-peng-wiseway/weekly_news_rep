Policy & Regulation

WA CIS Tender 11 opens, seeking 1.8 GW of wind and solar
pv magazine Australia · 2026-08-26 · Source
Key point: The federal government has opened Capacity Investment Scheme (CIS) Tender 11 for Western Australia’s Wholesale Electricity Market, seeking about 1.8 GW of new solar and wind generation from projects expected to operate by 2030. The tender is the first to require developers to have commenced a Developer Rating Scheme assessment, and includes a 180 MW First Nations Equity and Revenue Sharing Set Aside for projects offering at least 5% First Nations equity participation or an equivalent revenue-sharing arrangement.

Solar

Solmech completes 1.2 MWh DC-coupled storage retrofit at SA’s Warnertown Solar Farm
pv magazine Australia · 2026-08-26 · Source
Key point: Adelaide-headquartered Solmech completed a hybrid retrofit at the 4.1 MW Warnertown Solar Farm, about 215 km north of Adelaide, replacing six Sungrow PV inverters with hybrid units paired with 200 kWh PowerKeeper batteries, for 1.2 MWh of added storage. The DC-coupled configuration is intended to avoid transformer modifications and improve operating flexibility in South Australia’s wholesale market.

Battery Storage (BESS)

DC-coupled solar and storage projects can cut Australian grid-connection approvals by up to six months
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-08-26 · Source
Key point: At the Battery Asset Management Summit Australia 2026 in Sydney, Sungrow Australia’s Alistair Gibson said DC-coupled projects are assessed as one integrated solar-and-battery system and can reduce the Generator Performance Standards or connection process by up to six months compared with AC-coupled designs. He said the architecture can also lower capital expenditure by requiring fewer inverters.
HMC says equity is committed for its first large battery, with about 2 GW moving toward FID
RenewEconomy · 2026-08-26 · Source
Key point: HMC Capital says its Illuma Energy platform has committed equity for its first battery project and has about 2 GW of near-term renewable and storage developments moving toward final investment decision over the next few years. The first project is expected to be the 300 MW/1,200 MWh, approximately A$800 million Moorabool BESS in Victoria; KKR has committed up to A$248 million toward the first BESS, covering up to 90% of the platform’s first-project equity component.

Wind

Concrete pours begin for 38 turbine foundations at Queensland’s Boulder Creek wind project
RenewEconomy · 2026-08-26 · Source
Key point: Aula Energy has begun turbine-foundation pours at the 228 MW Boulder Creek wind project, 40 km south-west of Rockhampton in Central Queensland, alongside trenching, cabling, switching-station and road works. The 38-turbine project is expected to power more than 85,000 homes when fully operational and avoid about 379,000 tonnes of CO2 emissions annually.
