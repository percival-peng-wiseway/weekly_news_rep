Policy & Regulation

Victoria’s first offshore wind auction offers 20-year contracts and availability payments
Source: RenewEconomy | Date: 2026-10-06
URL: Original article
Key point: RenewEconomy’s 6 October report details Victoria’s first 2 GW offshore wind auction. The state guide confirms 20-year contracts for difference and periodic availability payments after commercial operation, conditional on generating availability. Proposals run from August 2026 to August 2027, with awards expected in 2028; construction from 2032 and first power in 2034 remain subject to tender and approval outcomes.
NSW reports regional conservation progress alongside Central-West Orana transmission
Source: NSW Government | Date: 2026-10-06
URL: Original article
Key point: On 6 October, NSW reported that more than 15,000 hectares across over 20 sites would be protected and restored alongside the Central-West Orana Renewable Energy Zone transmission project, whose expected disturbance is below 1,200 hectares. A regional Conservation Investment Strategy coordinates the work. The up-to-A$140 million conservation framework was introduced a year earlier; this is an implementation update, not a new funding allocation.

Solar

September rooftop solar adds 311.8 MW; home batteries near 1 GWh
Source: RenewEconomy | Date: 2026-10-06
URL: Original article
Key point: RenewEconomy reported SunWiz figures on 6 October showing 311.8 MW of rooftop solar added in September, with January–September additions 43% above the corresponding 2025 period. Estimated September home-battery additions reached 966.5 MWh, up 12.5% month-on-month. Average PV system size was 9.98 kW, below April’s 11.75 kW but slightly above August. These are September market figures, not capacity added on publication day.
September utility wind and solar generation reaches 5.44 TWh
Source: pv magazine Australia | Date: 2026-10-06
URL: Original article
Key point: In its 6 October report, pv magazine Australia cited Rystad Energy data showing 5.44 TWh of utility-scale wind and solar generation in September, up 7% year-on-year. NSW and Queensland utility solar reached September records of 841 GWh and 645 GWh. NEM utility-solar curtailment was 22% of available generation, down from 26% a year earlier. These are operating-performance figures, not new project commissioning.

Wind

EDF’s Balala wind and battery proposal opens for federal comment
Source: RenewEconomy | Date: 2026-10-06
URL: Original article
Key point: RenewEconomy reported on 6 October that EDF’s proposed Balala project near Uralla, NSW, comprises 60 turbines, 420 MW of wind and a 200 MW/800 MWh battery. The federal EPBC portal confirms the referral is open for public comment; the report gives a 20 October deadline. The project remains in early assessment, with construction envisaged from 2030 subject to approvals.

Company & Project Developments

YEC lodges development application for Baru Marnda renewables hub
Source: RenewEconomy | Date: 2026-10-06
URL: Original article
Key point: RenewEconomy reported on 6 October that Yindjibarndi Energy Corporation had lodged a development application for Baru Marnda in WA’s Pilbara. The Indigenous-led proposal comprises up to 1,000 MW of wind and 500 MW of solar, with an energy-storage option. Federal referral documents confirm those capacities, while the portal still shows additional information awaited. This is an application milestone, not approval, financial close or construction.

Infrastructure & Transmission

Updated AEMO outlook removes July 2028 low-reserve conditions in Victoria and SA
Source: WattClarity / AEMO market notice | Date: 2026-10-06
URL: Original article
Key point: WattClarity reproduced AEMO market notice 145486 on 6 October: the updated medium-term adequacy assessment identifies no low-reserve conditions, removing those previously forecast for July 2028 in Victoria and South Australia. The model incorporates updated Project EnergyConnect Stage 2 transmission capability. This concerns the September 2027–September 2028 assessment period and is a forecast, not a guarantee against future supply risks.
