Federal Cheaper Home Batteries Program passes 507,000 installations
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-08-17 · Source
Key point: Prime Minister Anthony Albanese and Energy Minister Chris Bowen announced that more than 507,000 households, small businesses and community organisations have installed batteries under the Cheaper Home Batteries Program. Launched in July 2025, the federal scheme discounts upfront costs for small batteries connected to solar by 30% and has an AUD 7.2 billion four-year budget.

ARENA awards AUD 6.52 million to UNSW for grid-forming storage and system-strength research
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-08-17 · Source
Key point: The Australian Renewable Energy Agency (ARENA) awarded the University of New South Wales AUD 6.52 million to study how inverter-based solar, wind and battery resources interact with protection systems in the National Electricity Market. The three-and-a-half-year PROFILES project will use hardware-in-the-loop testing to assess grid-forming batteries’ fault-current and system-strength role, and is scheduled to run until November 2030.
CATL confirmed as Tener S supplier for Quinbrook’s Supernode Stage 3 in Queensland
pv magazine Australia · 2026-08-17 · Source
Key point: CATL confirmed it will supply Tener S systems for Stage 3 of Quinbrook’s Supernode battery campus in south-east Queensland. The 260 MW/1,216 MWh stage has secured AUD 469 million in debt financing and a 15-year offtake agreement with Queensland government-owned generator Stanwell; the campus is planned at 780 MW/3,074 MWh overall.

Victoria approves the 122 MW Normanville wind farm
RenewEconomy · 2026-08-17 · Source
Key point: WestWind Energy has received Victorian development approval for the 122 MW Normanville Energy Park, with up to 17 turbines, south-west of Kerang in Gannawarra Shire. The project is in the North West Renewable Energy Zone and is planned to connect to Koorangie terminal station via a 7.2 km underground line, with operations targeted for 2028 and construction investment estimated at about AUD 350 million.
