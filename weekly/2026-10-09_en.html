Policy & Regulation

AER opens further consultation on 2026 network rate-of-return settings
Source: Australian Energy Regulator | Date: 2026-10-09
URL：Original article
Key point: The AER published a targeted discussion paper on equity beta and the market risk premium on 9 October, following its May draft decision and independent-panel review. Submissions close on 23 October; the regulator still targets December 2026 for its final Rate of Return Instrument. This is a consultation affecting regulated electricity and gas networks, not a final revenue allowance.
South Australian minister signals possible slippage in 100% net-renewables timing
Source: RenewEconomy | Date: 2026-10-09
URL：Original article
Key point: In reporting published on 9 October, Energy Minister Tom Koutsantonis acknowledged that South Australia’s 2027 target could slip into 2028. He retained confidence in reaching 100% net renewables. The comments came during that week’s Palmer wind-farm ceremony; they are a qualification of timing, rather than a verified formal change to the target.
Regional leaders call for funded capacity and coordinated renewable-project planning
Source: RE-Alliance | Date: 2026-10-09
URL：Original article
Key point: RE-Alliance released its Regional Leaders Summit report on 9 October, drawing on September discussions in Toowoomba involving more than 100 regional leaders. Recommendations include multi-year support for local capacity, coordinated planning for cumulative infrastructure impacts, and transparent community-benefit arrangements. These are stakeholder recommendations, not new government requirements or funding commitments.

Solar

Western Sydney Airport’s 4.5 MW rooftop solar delivery detailed
Source: pv magazine Australia / Trinasolar | Date: 2026-10-09
URL：Original article
Key point: The 9 October project report details Smart Commercial Energy’s installation of 8,824 Trinasolar modules across the airport terminal, piers and covered walkways, with estimated annual output of 5,800 MWh. The underlying Trinasolar release is dated 6 October, and CleanPeak already described the system as existing in July. This is delivery reporting, not confirmation of commissioning on 9 October; separate solar-and-battery expansion plans remain distinct.

Battery Storage (BESS)

Kojonup eight-hour battery concept gains conditional planning approval
Source: RenewEconomy / Mint Renewables | Date: 2026-10-09
URL：Original article
Key point: RenewEconomy’s 9 October coverage reports approval for Mint Renewables’ proposed Kojonup battery in Western Australia. The planning decision was made on 17 September. Mint describes an indicative capacity of up to 800 MWh, with 100 MW over eight hours an example configuration; final sizing, remaining approvals and construction are still ahead.
Household study links batteries and time-of-use tariffs with lower peak imports
Source: RenewEconomy / Victoria Energy Policy Centre | Date: 2026-10-09
URL：Original article
Key point: October 9 coverage of a September VEPC working paper describes a three-year study of 5,096 households across four states. Solar-plus-battery households on time-of-use tariffs imported about 13% less electricity at peak times than comparable non-solar households, while wider peak/off-peak price gaps showed no detectable extra response. The observational findings are not a guarantee of individual bill savings.

Wind

Palmer’s 288 MW wind-farm construction milestone receives new coverage
Source: Energy Source & Distribution / Tilt Renewables | Date: 2026-10-09
URL：Original article
Key point: Energy Source & Distribution’s 9 October report covers Tilt Renewables’ Palmer Wind Farm, around 70 km east of Adelaide, and its formal sod-turn on 7 October. Tilt says construction began earlier in 2026 and expects more than 200 construction jobs. This is coverage of an earlier ceremony at an under-construction project, not a construction start on 9 October.
