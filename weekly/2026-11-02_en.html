Policy & regulation

Study models higher electricity-sector employment under a fossil-fuel phase-out
Climate Analytics · 2026-11-02
Original report
Climate Analytics published a workforce scenario study on 2 November. Its 1.5°C-aligned fossil-fuel phase-out pathway models annual national power-sector labour demand rising from 37,000 full-time-equivalent jobs in 2025 to 76,000 in 2035 and 87,000 in 2050. New jobs would not necessarily be located where fossil-fuel employment is lost, requiring training and regional workforce planning. These are conditional modelling results, not jobs already created or a newly adopted government target.

Solar

Risen outlines RSM90 rooftop module and conditional 40-year warranty
Risen Energy Australia · 2026-11-02
Original report
Risen’s 2 November product announcement describes its all-black RSM90 HJT rooftop module for Australia and New Zealand, rated at 460–500 Wp with claimed peak efficiency of 23.5%. The 40-year product and linear-power cover combines base terms of 25 and 30 years with free extensions of 15 and 10 years respectively. Eligibility applies to qualifying rooftop modules first installed after 15 October 2026 and remains subject to full warranty terms. This is a manufacturer announcement, not independently verified field performance.

Battery storage

SEC signs ten-year Glenrowan solar and battery offtake
SEC Victoria · 2026-11-02
Original report
SEC announced a new ten-year solar and battery offtake agreement with Pacific Partnerships on 2 November, replacing the previous arrangement. It supports a planned 102 MW, four-hour battery beside the existing Glenrowan solar farm. Construction is expected after financial close: the offtake announcement does not establish that financing is complete, construction has started or the battery is operating. It should not be confused with other batteries in the Glenrowan area.
Sungrow presents home battery system with integrated EV charging
pv magazine Australia · 2026-11-02
Original report
A 2 November report describes Sungrow’s PowerHarbour Pro residential system, integrating inverter, battery storage and AC electric-vehicle charging. Australian CEC approval is still pending, with installation availability expected in the first quarter of 2027. It is therefore a product rollout plan, not an approved or operating installation. Future V2X functionality should not be treated as a currently guaranteed service.

Companies & projects

Rest lifts total MPower commitment to A$250 million
Rest · 2026-11-02
Original report
Rest announced on 2 November that its total commitment to MPower rises to A$250 million, rather than an additional A$250 million. Together with Wollemi capital, funding totals A$350 million for the mid-scale solar and storage strategy. The announcement says projects are under construction in three states. A capital commitment is distinct from full deployment of the money or operation of the entire project pipeline.
Clutch secures ARENA support for portable-battery pilot manufacturing
pv magazine Australia / Australian Government · 2026-11-02
Original report · Official announcement · 2026-10-30
Industry coverage published on 2 November follows an official funding announcement on 30 October. Clutch receives A$6.77 million from ARENA for a A$19.35 million pilot-line project in Coburg North. The work will design, build and commission scalable assembly and testing capability for modular portable batteries, with at least 16 ongoing jobs expected. This is a manufacturing-development project, not an already operating mass-production factory.

Grid & related infrastructure

Western Power issues Gnangara transmission construction notice
Western Power · 2026-11-02
Original report
A Western Power work notice dated 2 November schedules GenusPlus transmission construction in the Gnangara Off-Road Vehicle Area, with an expected start that day and completion by December 2027. Activities include temporary access tracks, tower assembly and installation areas, and traffic separation. The works form part of the 28 km, 330 kV Northern Terminal–Neerabup Terminal line, intended to support more renewable generation between Perth and the Mid West. The notice describes a works schedule, not energisation or the first start of the entire project.
