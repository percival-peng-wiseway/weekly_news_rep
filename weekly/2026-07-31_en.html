Policy & Regulation

NSW and Queensland Take Divergent Energy Paths: NSW Advances Renewable Energy Zones While Queensland Extends Coal and Emphasises Gas

RenewEconomy · 2026-07-31 · Source

At the Australian Clean Energy Summit, NSW Energy Minister Penny Sharpe said the state is pursuing wind, solar, storage, transmission and Renewable Energy Zones to replace ageing coal generation. Queensland Energy Minister David Janetzki stressed the continued use of the state's government-owned energy assets, coal and gas resources. Citing Open Electricity, the report puts coal's share of generation over the prior 12 months at about 60% in Queensland and 53% in NSW.

Industry Commentary Calls for the Federal Capacity Investment Scheme to Move to a Contract-for-Difference Model

RenewEconomy · 2026-07-31 · Source

An industry commentary argues that the current Capacity Investment Scheme (CIS) does not provide sufficient revenue certainty for equity financing, leaving utility-scale wind and solar projects struggling to reach final investment decision. It proposes fixed contracts for difference (CfDs) to provide clearer long-term revenue support. This is the author's opinion, not a government policy change.

Battery Storage (BESS)

Neoen Launches Goyder Battery Stage 2, Taking the South Australian Project to 454MW / 1,814MWh

Australian Rural & Regional News (reprinting Neoen's 29 July media release) · 2026-07-31 · Source

Neoen has secured two 15-year capacity service contracts in South Australia's first Firm Energy Reliability Mechanism tender, each for 75MW and requiring eight hours of continuous dispatchability. It has executed Stage 2 contracts with Tesla and UGL for 227MW / 907MWh; together with Stage 1, Goyder Battery will total 454MW / 1,814MWh. Site works are due to begin in August 2026, with operations targeted for 2028.

1414 Degrees Signs Agreement for up to 1GW AI Data Centre at South Australia's Aurora Energy Precinct

Energy-Storage.news · 2026-07-31 · Source

1414 Degrees has granted an unnamed data-centre operator exclusivity over an initial 40-hectare parcel at Aurora Energy Precinct for an AI data-centre development of up to 1GW. Near Port Augusta, the site has potential for up to 900MW of solar, an approved 140MW / 280MWh BESS, and transmission, fibre and water access. The first proposed stage is a 17MW campus using the existing 33kV connection.

Gippsland's First 2GW Offshore Wind Auction Is Due in August, but Costs and Policy Uncertainty Remain

RenewEconomy · 2026-07-31 · Source

Victoria's first 2GW Gippsland offshore-wind capacity auction is expected to launch in August. Industry participants say Australian offshore wind still faces high costs, distance from global supply chains, uncertainty around coal closures and limited bipartisan support. The article notes that about 9GW of offshore wind was installed globally last year, with a further 328GW from committed or under-construction projects expected to be operating by 2035.

Company & Project Developments

Denmark's Transition Offers Lessons for Australian Project Delivery: Community Participation and Approval Certainty

RenewEconomy · 2026-07-31 · Source

An industry commentary uses Denmark as a reference point and argues that community benefit sharing, early local participation, coordinated approvals and transparent grid-connection pathways should be built into Australian energy projects. It cites Eurostat data showing renewables accounted for 88.4% of Denmark's net electricity generation in 2024. The proposed lessons are the author's views for Australian policy and project-development discussion.
