Public submissions highlighted for proposed 200 MW / 800 MWh Darnum battery
Source: Warragul & Drouin Gazette / Planning Victoria | Date: 2026-10-11
URL：Original article
Key point: The Gazette’s 11 October report highlights public submissions on Samsung C&T Renewable Energy Australia’s proposed Darnum battery at Shady Creek, Victoria. The planning register records receipt of the application on 2 July and public notice on 2 September, with no decision listed. The project remains a proposal; developer material anticipates construction in 2027 for about 18 months, subject to approval, with connection to AusNet’s 220 kV transmission line.
