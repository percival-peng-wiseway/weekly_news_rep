Policy & Regulation

Australia advances renewable-energy and storage compliance framework for large data centres
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-08-11 · Source
Key point: The Australian Energy Market Commission proposed four measures requiring large data centres to match consumption with certificates linked to new renewables, contract firming capacity, and register eligible facilities as National Electricity Market participants. New South Wales proposes a renewable-energy-zone-style grid-access scheme for large loads of 5 MW or more; federal legislation is expected in early 2027.
CEFC records AUD 9.1 billion in FY26 commitments, catalysing AUD 19.6 billion in transaction value
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-08-11 · Source
Key point: The Clean Energy Finance Corporation committed AUD 9.1 billion in FY26 to 30 June 2026, completing 45 new transactions and supporting AUD 19.6 billion in total transaction value; lifetime catalysed clean-energy project value reached AUD 105 billion. Its Rewiring the Nation Fund committed AUD 7.2 billion, including AUD 3.8 billion for Stage 1 of the 750 MW Marinus Link.

ClearVue 600 W rooftop solar panel gains Clean Energy Council approval
pv magazine Australia · 2026-08-11 · Source
Key point: Perth-based ClearVue Technologies’ ClearVue-Helios rooftop module has gained Clean Energy Council approval, enabling grid connection and eligibility for government incentives. Zenith seeks electricity-sale licence for wind, solar and storage hybrid serving Kalgoorlie Super Pit
RenewEconomy · 2026-08-11 · Source
Key point: Zenith Energy proposes the Eastern Goldfields Power Project for the Kalgoorlie Super Pit gold-mine joint ventures in Western Australia: 256 MW of wind, 110 MWac of solar and a 138 MW/300 MWh battery, targeting about 70% renewable supply.
