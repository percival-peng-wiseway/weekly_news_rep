Battery Storage

Akaysha completes A$1.75 billion battery portfolio refinancing
Source: Akaysha Energy | Date: 2026-10-20
URL: Original source
Key point: Akaysha announced on 20 October that it had consolidated debt for Brendale, Orana, Ulinda Park and Elaine into a A$1.75 billion portfolio facility backed by 11 banks, with five-, seven- and ten-year terms. Elaine remains under construction, targeting completion in the second half of 2027. This is a refinancing milestone, not simultaneous commissioning of four batteries; Waratah is not among the refinanced assets.
ATMOCE extends Australian 7 kWh home-battery warranty to 15 years
Source: ATMOCE / PR Newswire | Date: 2026-10-20
URL: Original source
Key point: ATMOCE announced on 20 October that its Australian standard warranty for the M-ELV 7 kWh battery (MS-7K-U) would rise from 12 to 15 years, including units already shipped to Australia. Coverage runs from the original warranty start date. The stated limits are 15 years or 29.2 MWh of throughput, whichever comes first, with at least 60% state of health retained. This is a manufacturer warranty change, not an unconditional performance guarantee.

Wind

Bungaban 1.4 GW wind project reportedly seeks at least A$1.5 billion
Source: RenewEconomy | Date: 2026-10-20
URL: Original source
Key point: RenewEconomy reported on 20 October, citing the Australian Financial Review’s Street Talk column, that Windlab and Squadron Energy’s Bungaban wind project was seeking at least A$1.5 billion against a reported A$5 billion project cost. The new information concerns fundraising, distinct from the environmental approval previously covered. It does not establish financial close or construction commencement; the amounts are media-reported, not a same-day developer announcement of secured investment.

Company & Project Developments

Four Directions Fund commits to advancing up to five Indigenous-led Australian projects
Source: Global Citizen / Medianet | Date: 2026-10-20
URL: Original source · Additional reporting
Key point: At Global Citizen NOW: Perth on 20 October, Four Directions Fund committed to partnering with the First Nations Clean Energy Network to advance up to five Indigenous-led renewable-energy projects in Australia, aiming for at least 10% Indigenous ownership. This is a development commitment, not construction or commissioning. Its separate ambition for 1 GW of projects to be ready to build by 2029 spans Australia, New Zealand and the United States, rather than Australia alone.

Infrastructure & Transmission

Uralla Council seeks longer exhibition for New England REZ transmission assessment
Source: New England Times | Date: 2026-10-20
URL: Original source
Key point: New England Times reported on 20 October that Uralla Shire Council had asked the NSW Planning Minister for at least four extra weeks to review the transmission project’s environmental impact statement, preferably extending exhibition to 31 December. This is a new consultation request, not a repeat of the 15 October exhibition launch. The department’s response still directed submissions by 12 November; an extension had not been confirmed.
