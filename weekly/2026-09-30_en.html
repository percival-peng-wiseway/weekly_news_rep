Policy & Regulation

AER consults on Essential Energy’s islandable microgrid trial
Source: Australian Energy Regulator | Date: 2026-09-30
URL: Original article
Key point: The AER opened consultation on regulatory sandbox waivers sought by Essential Energy to test islandable microgrids. These would normally remain grid-connected but could supply communities independently during outages. The trial would examine resilience, generation and storage ownership, technical operation and market interfaces; submissions close on 12 November. Approval has not yet been granted.
AEMO proposes changes to WA commissioning-test assessments
Source: AEMO | Date: 2026-09-30
URL: Original article
Key point: AEMO opened consultation on changes to the WEM Commissioning Tests procedure. The proposal would enable advice from the network operator when assessing commissioning plans and clarify how existing or new reactive-power studies are considered. Submissions close at 5pm AWST on 28 October; the proposed commencement date is 2 November, subject to the consultation process.

Solar

NSW funds rooftop solar upgrade at Queanbeyan Hospital
Source: NSW Health | Date: 2026-09-30
URL: Original article
Key point: NSW announced more than A$810,000 for minor capital works at Queanbeyan Hospital under the Regional Health Minor Works Program. The investment will support a roof-mounted solar installation to reduce reliance on grid electricity and help meet emissions-reduction targets. The release does not give the system’s capacity or completion date.
Eurobodalla adopts emissions plan and funds solar installations
Source: Eurobodalla Shire Council | Date: 2026-09-30
URL: Original article
Key point: Eurobodalla councillors adopted the Emissions Reduction Plan 2025–2040 and associated budgets at their 29 September meeting, reported on 30 September. These include A$1.1 million for solar at Bay Pavilions and Narooma Pool in 2026–27, an ongoing A$200,000 annual solar budget, and EV charging infrastructure at council premises.

Battery Storage (BESS)

FRV commissions 100 MW / 200 MWh Terang battery
Source: FRV Australia / pv magazine Australia | Date: 2026-09-30
URL: Original article
Key point: FRV Australia announced successful commissioning of the 100 MW / 200 MWh Terang battery in southwest Victoria, its first standalone utility-scale battery in Australia. The project received A$7 million from Victoria’s Energy Innovation Fund to support grid-forming inverter technology. It adds dispatchable storage to FRV’s Australian generation portfolio.
Stoney Creek 125 MW / 1 GWh battery receives NSW development approval
Source: Energy Vault | Date: 2026-09-29
URL: Original article
Key point: Energy Vault announced State Significant Development Approval for the 125 MW / 1 GWh Stoney Creek battery near Narrabri. Site works and mobilisation are expected in the fourth quarter of 2026, with commercial operation targeted for the first half of 2028, subject to remaining conditions. The eight-hour battery has a 14-year Long-Term Energy Service Agreement. The release was issued at 10:35am EDT on 29 September, corresponding to 12:35am AEST on 30 September.

Wind

Kondinin’s 130 MW first stage reaches financial close
Source: Foresight Group | Date: 2026-09-30
URL: Original article
Key point: Foresight announced financial close for the 130 MW first stage of Kondinin Wind Farm in Western Australia, securing A$430 million in debt. It also acquired Shell Energy’s 50% interest in the wider 370 MW development. Stage 1 comprises 21 Vestas turbines and is backed by a seven-year Synergy power purchase agreement and 15-year Capacity Investment Scheme support; first electricity is targeted for late 2028.

Company & Project Developments

Sicona seeks A$55 million in equity for battery-materials plant
Source: Sicona / Third Hemisphere via EINPresswire | Date: 2026-09-30
URL: Original article
Key point: In a 30 September company-issued release, Sicona called for faster late-stage manufacturing finance. It said its proposed Port Kembla silicon-carbon anode facility had A$45 million in ARENA support but still required A$55 million in equity. Planned annual capacity is 230 tonnes; the additional equity was not secured.

Infrastructure & Transmission

AER publishes A$385.6 million revenue decision for NSW system-strength project
Source: Australian Energy Regulator | Date: 2026-09-30
URL: Original article
Key point: The AER published its final revenue determination for Transgrid’s System Strength Project, which includes ten synchronous condensers at five sites. It allows A$385.6 million in nominal revenue over 2026–31, A$15.2 million below Transgrid’s proposal. The equipment is intended to support voltage and system stability as NSW’s generation mix changes.
AER proposes A$5.694 billion Powerlink revenue allowance for 2027–32
Source: Australian Energy Regulator | Date: 2026-09-30
URL: Original article
Key point: The AER’s draft determination proposes A$5.6941 billion in allowed Powerlink revenue for 2027–32, 0.3% below its proposal. It also proposes a A$1.3595 billion reduction in forecast capital expenditure, citing insufficient supporting evidence. Powerlink can respond with a revised proposal by 2 December; the final determination is due by 30 April 2027.
Western Power activates dynamic line ratings on selected lines
Source: Western Power | Date: 2026-09-30
URL: Original article
Key point: Western Power announced live dynamic line rating on selected transmission lines, using real-time weather and solar irradiance to adjust safe capacity. Results will guide wider deployment, with potential to accommodate more renewable generation and evening demand. The rollout is selective, not network-wide.
