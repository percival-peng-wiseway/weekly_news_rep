Policy & Regulation

Cheaper Home Batteries Program passes 507,000 installations
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-08-17 · Source
Key point: Prime Minister Anthony Albanese and Energy Minister Chris Bowen said more than 507,000 households, small businesses and community organisations had installed batteries through the Cheaper Home Batteries Program. The program began in July 2025, provides a 30% upfront discount for small batteries connected to solar, and has a four-year AU$7.2 billion budget.
NSW offers upfront battery discounts of up to 40% for businesses and apartments
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-08-18 · Source
Key point: New South Wales launched an upfront discount program for battery storage systems in businesses and apartment buildings. The discount can reduce costs by up to 40% for those installation settings.
Rewiring Australia and Google launch free household-electrification AI tool
pv magazine Australia · 2026-08-18 · Source
Key point: Rewiring Australia and Google launched a free AI tool intended to help Australian households electrify. The report places the initiative in the residential-market and policy context.
Victoria's first offshore-wind auction faces a narrowing pre-election timetable
RenewEconomy · 2026-08-19 · Source
Key point: Victoria had committed to hold Australia's first offshore-wind auction in August. Officials still said the auction would proceed, but the approaching state election narrows the available timeframe and affects the next steps for local offshore-wind projects.
AER says battery storage is reshaping the National Electricity Market
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-08-20 · Source
Key point: The Australian Energy Regulator said battery energy storage systems are reshaping the National Electricity Market. The issue concerns the effect of storage assets on the way the market operates.

Solar

UNSW researchers build 12.6%-efficient kesterite solar cell
pv magazine Australia · 2026-08-18 · Source
Key point: University of New South Wales researchers built a kesterite solar cell with 12.6% power-conversion efficiency and lower open-circuit-voltage losses. The result was reported on 18 August as a photovoltaic-cell research advance.
Australian researchers develop automated screening for decommissioned PV modules
pv magazine Australia · 2026-08-19 · Source
Key point: Australian researchers developed an automated screening method for decommissioned photovoltaic modules. It focuses on testing and sorting modules before reuse.
Windlab and Squadron submit 500 MW solar and 500 MW battery project for federal assessment
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-08-20 · Source
Key point: A Windlab–Squadron Energy joint venture submitted the Bungaban project for EPBC Act assessment. The proposal comprises 500 MW of solar and a 500 MW BESS near Wandoan in Queensland's Western Downs, about 50 km north-east of the town; construction is proposed for July 2028 and the line would connect to Powerlink's proposed Warranna substation.

Battery Storage (BESS)

ARENA awards AU$6.52 million to UNSW for grid-forming storage and system-strength research
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-08-17 · Source
Key point: ARENA awarded AU$6.52 million to UNSW to study how inverter-based resources, including solar, wind and batteries, interact with National Electricity Market protection systems. The three-and-a-half-year PROFILES project will use hardware-in-the-loop testing to assess grid-forming batteries for fault current and system-strength services and is planned to run to November 2030.
CATL confirms Tener S supply for Quinbrook's Supernode Stage 3
pv magazine Australia · 2026-08-17 · Source
Key point: CATL confirmed it will supply Tener S systems for Stage 3 of Quinbrook's Supernode battery park in south-east Queensland. The 260 MW/1,216 MWh stage has AU$469 million in debt financing and a 15-year offtake agreement with state-owned generator Stanwell; the park is planned to total 780 MW/3,074 MWh.
PLUS Grid Storage breaks ground on 200 MW/400 MWh Steel River East BESS
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-08-18 · Source
Key point: Ausgrid subsidiary PLUS Grid Storage broke ground on the Steel River East battery project in Newcastle, New South Wales. The project is sized at 200 MW/400 MWh.
Origin's 650 MWh Mortlake grid-forming BESS begins commercial operation
pv magazine Australia · 2026-08-19 · Source
Key point: Origin's 650 MWh grid-forming battery energy storage system at Mortlake, Victoria, began commercial operations. The system uses 1,044 Fluence GridStack battery units, 62 transformers and 116 SMA grid-forming inverters.
EtaPRO deploys digital twin at MGA Thermal's 5 MWh Tomago thermal-storage demonstration
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-08-19 · Source
Key point: EtaPRO deployed digital-twin and predictive-analytics technology at MGA Thermal's 5 MWh thermal-storage demonstration project in Tomago. The system is intended for operational analysis at the long-duration energy-storage demonstration facility.
APA approves AU$259 million investment in 72 MW solar and 52 MW/104 MWh BESS for Ernest Henry mine
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-08-20 · Source
Key point: APA made a final investment decision to build, own and operate the 72 MW Sybella Creek solar farm and a 52 MW/104 MWh BESS at Mount Isa, Queensland, for AU$259 million. A power supply agreement with Evolution Mining's Ernest Henry Mining runs to mid-2046; construction is expected to begin in late 2026 and complete in mid-2028.

Key point: Renewable developer Squadron Energy, through Squadron Energy Retail Pty Ltd, applied to Victoria's Essential Services Commission for an electricity retail licence to supply medium and large business customers across the state.
