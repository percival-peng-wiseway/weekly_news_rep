Solar

Warradarge proposal adds up to 500 MW solar and 500 MW battery at estimated A$2 billion
Source: RenewEconomy | Date: 2026-10-05
URL: Original article
Key point: RenewEconomy reported SynergyRED’s proposed addition of up to 500 MW of solar and 500 MW of battery power within Warradarge Wind Farm on 5 October. A 2 October Shire of Coorow notice confirms the approximately A$2 billion development application, with submissions closing at 4 pm on 2 November. It remains in feasibility and approvals; the verified sources do not specify battery energy capacity or duration.

Battery Storage (BESS)

Origin proposes 500 MW / 4,942 MWh Eraring North battery, subject to approvals
Source: RenewEconomy | Date: 2026-10-05
URL: Original article
Key point: RenewEconomy reported on Origin’s Eraring North proposal on 5 October. Its federal environmental referral specifies an additional 500 MW / approximately 4,942 MWh, taking combined Eraring battery capacity to around 1,200 MW / 8,600 MWh, subject to detailed design. The project remains in early approvals, targeting construction in 2028 and commercial operations in early-to-mid 2031, subject to regulatory approvals.

Company & Project Developments

Energy Bay–Centuria agreement plans 70 MW rooftop solar and 300 MWh storage
Source: pv magazine Australia | Date: 2026-10-05
URL: Original article
Key point: pv magazine’s 5 October report covers Energy Bay’s agreement with Centuria, originally announced on 2 October. Energy Bay plans approximately A$430 million of investment to deploy about 70 MW of solar and 300 MWh of batteries across up to 240 properties over seven years, with 30-year embedded-network arrangements. Rollout is scheduled to start in October; new installations remain subject to tenant consent, planning approvals and due diligence.

Infrastructure & Transmission

Evoenergy to support Canberra electrification trial without joining as a formal partner
Source: RenewEconomy | Date: 2026-10-05
URL: Original article
Key point: RenewEconomy reported on 5 October that Evoenergy declined a formal partnership in Go Electric Canberra but will share relevant network information and handle connections. ARENA lists the trial’s start date as 9 September 2026 and targets of 1,000 households and 200 vehicle-to-grid installations. It will test household electrification and flexible demand in a cold climate; the new reporting concerns the network operator’s participation.
