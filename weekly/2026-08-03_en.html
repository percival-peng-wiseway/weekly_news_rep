Policy & Regulation

EnergyAustralia Admits Electricity Retail Code Breach over Delayed Solar Sharer Offer
ESD News · 2026-08-03 · Source
Key point: EnergyAustralia admitted it failed to make its Solar Sharer Offer available from 1 July 2026 to eligible households in New South Wales, South Australia and south-east Queensland, breaching the Electricity Retail Code. The offer provides three hours of free daytime electricity. The retailer launched the offer on 3 August; the ACCC accepted a court-enforceable undertaking requiring it to keep the offer available and report compliance to the ACCC and Australian Energy Regulator.

Battery Storage (BESS)

AEMO Launches Fault-Current Trial as Grid-Forming BESS Status against Highest System-Strength Standard Remains Unconfirmed
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-08-03 · Source
Key point: AEMO says grid-forming BESS have not yet been confirmed to meet the highest tier of minimum system-strength requirements in Australia’s National Electricity Market and has launched a fault-current trial. The trial will assess the role of inverter-based resources in system strength and grid-fault response; Victoria’s Koorangie BESS uses 100 Tesla Megapacks with grid-forming inverters.

AusNet Receives Federal Grid-Enhancing Technology Funding to Trial Greater Capacity from Victoria’s Existing Network
ESD News · 2026-08-03 · Source
Key point: AusNet received funding through the Australian Government’s A$30 million Grid Enhancing Technologies program for its Network Exchanger (NEx) project, one of 14 newly announced funded projects, aimed at unlocking more capacity from Victoria’s existing distribution network. AusNet is also supporting EcoJoule’s EcoVAR V2, JET Charge’s vehicle-to-grid and RMIT’s FlexBat trials to improve power quality, rooftop-solar hosting capacity and coordinated battery use.
