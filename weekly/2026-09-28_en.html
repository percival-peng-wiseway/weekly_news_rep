Policy & Regulation

Governments fund A$11 million Illawarra social-housing virtual power plant pilot
Source: Australian Government / NSW Government | Date: 2026-09-28
URL: Original article
Key point: The Commonwealth will contribute A$10 million and NSW A$1 million to install solar and batteries at selected social-housing properties in the Illawarra Renewable Energy Zone and connect them through a virtual power plant. Market sounding is open to installers and VPP operators until 5 pm on 25 October 2026 to inform programme design.

Battery Storage (BESS)

850 MW / 1,680 MWh Waratah Super Battery reaches full operations
Source: EnergyCo / NSW Government | Date: 2026-09-28
URL: Original article
Key point: EnergyCo confirmed that the Waratah Super Battery at the former Munmorah power-station site is fully operational following AEMO approval to operate at full capacity. Built by Akaysha Energy, the 850 MW / 1,680 MWh battery is part of a system integrity protection scheme that responds to major network disturbances and supports greater use of existing transmission capacity.
Australian Powerwall 2 owners gain Powerwall 3 expansion compatibility
Source: pv magazine Australia | Date: 2026-09-28
URL: Original article
Key point: Reporting on 28 September covered Tesla’s confirmation that eligible Australian Powerwall 2 systems can operate with Powerwall 3 using software version 26.26 or later and certified installation. Tesla’s announcement was made on 23 September; Gateway 1 and Neurio meters are not supported, so this is not an unconditional upgrade for every existing system.

Wind

CS Energy explores equity investment in 285 MW Lotus Creek; no deal signed
Source: Renewables Now | Date: 2026-09-28
URL: Original article
Key point: Renewables Now reported on 28 September that CS Energy was working with QIC’s Investor Gateway on potential equity investment in the 285 MW Lotus Creek wind farm, with no transaction entered into. CS Energy’s project information confirms the Central Queensland wind farm remains under construction with operations expected in 2027; potential investment is not a completed sale.

Company & Project Developments

Gamuda and Yanara sign Mortlake Energy Hub Stage 1 EPC contract
Source: Gamuda Australia | Date: 2026-09-28
URL: Original article
Key point: Gamuda Australia and Yanara signed the engineering, procurement and construction contract for Stage 1 in western Victoria, combining 435 MW of solar with a 300 MW, 4.5-hour battery. It will connect through Mortlake Terminal Station to the existing 500 kV network; main construction is scheduled for November 2026, energisation for April 2028 and operations for the first quarter of 2029.

Infrastructure & Transmission

Powerlink appoints Zinfra for Central Queensland transmission reinforcement
Source: Powerlink | Date: 2026-09-28
URL: Original article
Key point: Powerlink appointed Zinfra to build the transmission line from Calvale Substation to the new Gladstone West Substation and upgrade Calvale. Clearing and access works have begun as part of reinforcement intended to strengthen reliability, meet growing demand and support new renewable generation; the original announcement was published on 28 September.
