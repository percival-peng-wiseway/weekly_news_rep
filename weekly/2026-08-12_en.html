Battery Storage (BESS)

Australian battery storage sets 4,325 MW NEM discharge record
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-08-12 · Source
Key point: At 18:25 on 11 August, battery discharge in the National Electricity Market (NEM) reached 4,325 MW, exceeding the 4,131 MW record set on 31 July; batteries supplied 14.42% of NEM consumption at the same interval. The fleet shifted from nearly 4 GW of daytime charging to more than 4.3 GW of evening discharge, an intraday operating swing of roughly 8.3 GW.
Octopus Australia advances 64 MW/128 MWh DC-coupled battery at Victoria’s Fulham project
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-08-12 · Source
Key point: Octopus Australia has completed installation of the 80 MW solar array at its Fulham Solar & Battery project in Victoria, comprising 151,020 modules, and is moving to construction of a 64 MW/128 MWh DC-coupled battery. Wärtsilä will supply the battery, which is expected on site early in the second half of 2026; operations are due in 2027.
EPEC wins high-voltage grid-connection contract for Queensland’s Lower Wonga solar-plus-storage project
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-08-12 · Source
Key point: Engaged by EPC contractor INTEC Energy Solutions, EPEC Group will build, test and commission a 275/33 kV substation and 275 kV overhead line connecting the Lower Wonga Solar Farm in Queensland’s Gympie region to the Woolooga Powerlink substation. The project combines 380 MWdc of solar PV with a 281 MW/843 MWh battery energy storage system.
