Policy & Regulation

Victoria cancels the Central-North Renewable Energy Zone, citing limited grid capacity
RenewEconomy · 2026-09-10 · Source
Key point: The Victorian government has cancelled the proposed Central-North Renewable Energy Zone (REZ), which covered two areas between Bendigo and Glenrowan along a 220kV line, citing substantial existing development, no planned new transmission and limited remaining grid capacity. VicGrid says solar projects already seeking connection may use the remaining capacity before the REZ regime starts next year; well-advanced projects outside REZs with approvals or federal underwriting can use transitional arrangements.
Federal government reportedly plans departmental oversight of consumer-energy technical regulation
RenewEconomy · 2026-09-10 · Source
Key point: The federal government is reportedly seeking support at the 11 September Energy and Climate Ministerial Council meeting for a Technical Requirements Office within the Department of Climate Change, Energy, the Environment and Water (DCCEEW) to oversee rooftop solar, household batteries and EVs, rather than the Clean Energy Regulator previously identified as the preferred agency. Consumer-energy advocates raised concerns about transparency and the absence of public consultation on the technical standards.
Victorian solar installer fined AU$10,000 over false statutory declarations
RenewEconomy · 2026-09-10 · Source
Key point: The Clean Energy Regulator said installer Toby Payne pleaded guilty in the Frankston Magistrates’ Court to intentionally making a false statutory declaration and was convicted and fined AU$10,000. He claimed to have attended Melbourne solar installations while interstate; the declarations were used to create Small-scale Technology Certificates, and the regulator says it has suspended or removed five registered persons and two accredited installers in the past year.

Winton North solar-and-storage project in Victoria installs 200,172 modules
RenewEconomy · 2026-09-10 · Source
Key point: European Energy says all 200,172 modules are installed at the 131MW Winton North Solar Farm, about 22km southwest of Wangaratta, Victoria; the project is entering testing and targets grid connection next year. Its co-located 100MW/220MWh battery is expected online by winter 2027, while the solar plant is forecast to generate about 251GWh annually.
ICC Sydney to host the Southern Hemisphere’s largest CBD rooftop solar array
PVTech · 2026-09-10 · Source
Key point: The NSW government says ICC Sydney will expand its rooftop solar network to more than 18,400 square metres, with estimated annual generation of 2.6GWh, making it the largest rooftop solar installation in any Southern Hemisphere central business district. Combined with renewable-energy procurement, the expanded array is intended to allow the convention venue to run on 100% renewable energy.

Battery Storage (BESS)

Waratah Super Battery tests 850MW full output and 700MW SIPS service
Energy-Storage.news · 2026-09-10 · Source
Key point: Open Electricity data shows Akaysha Energy’s Waratah Super Battery near Budgewoi, NSW briefly reached its 850MW rated output on 7 September and discharged 701MW on 9 September, consistent with its contracted 700MW System Integrity Protection Scheme (SIPS) service for Transgrid. Rated at 850MW/1,680MWh, its SIPS control system monitors 36 transmission lines in real time to increase usable capacity on the Hunter, Sydney and Illawarra network ahead of the Hunter Transmission Project.
Iberdrola withdraws planning application for Queensland’s 2,000MWh Gin Gin battery
RenewEconomy · 2026-09-10 · Source
Key point: Iberdrola has withdrawn the planning application for its proposed Gin Gin battery in Queensland, a 500MW, up-to-four-hour (2,000MWh) project whose assessment was called in by Planning Minister Jarrod Bleijie in April. The company says it remains committed and is working with Bundaberg Regional Council on a social-impact assessment and community-benefit agreement, aiming to complete negotiations by the end of 2026 before resubmitting.
