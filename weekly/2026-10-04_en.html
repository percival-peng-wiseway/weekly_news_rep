Battery Storage (BESS)

195-tonne transformer delivery advances Bulabul battery’s energisation preparations
Source: RenewEconomy | Date: 2026-10-04
URL: Original article
Key point: RenewEconomy reported that a 195-tonne Siemens transformer reached AMPYR’s Bulabul battery near Wellington, NSW, in mid-September after a 1,300 km journey. High-voltage installation, testing and commissioning preparations are progressing. The two-stage project totals a planned 400 MW / 1 GWh; delivery does not establish commercial operation.

Macorna receives environmental-report pathway; alternative connections remain unconfirmed
Source: RenewEconomy | Date: 2026-10-04
URL: Original article
Key point: RenewEconomy reported that Victoria’s proposed Macorna wind farm will follow an environmental-report pathway rather than a full EES. Atmos is investigating alternatives to VNI West, including an existing 220 kV line, but viability is unconfirmed. The current proposal has up to 114 turbines; the pathway decision is not final construction approval.
