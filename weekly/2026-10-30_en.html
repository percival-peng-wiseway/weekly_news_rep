Policy and regulation

Corporate renewable buyers face a contracting squeeze
Source: RenewEconomy | Date: 2026-10-30
URL: Original article
Summary: RenewEconomy reports 6 GW of corporate renewable contracting in 2026, including a 3 GW Tomago arrangement whose wind and solar suppliers remained unresolved. Speakers warned that sustainability deadlines, expiring contracts and data-centre demand could intensify competition. The volume is attributed reporting, not independently verified against the new primary report; it is neither commissioned capacity nor a new regulatory obligation.

Solar

Solar recycling brief calls for a national framework
Source: pv magazine Australia / RAND | Date: 2026-10-30
URL: Original article · Supporting source
Summary: Reporting on 30 October highlights a RAND brief published on 27 October. It projects cumulative Australian PV waste, including historical waste, at 1.1 million tonnes by 2035. Recommendations include a national circularity framework by mid-2027, commercial-scale reuse and recycling support by 2028, and certification and trading mechanisms. These are research recommendations and forecasts, not enacted recycling laws.

Battery storage

CLOU receives Aqua-C conformity report
Source: pv magazine Australia / CLOU company release | Date: 2026-10-30
URL: Original article · Supporting source
Summary: Coverage on 30 October reports that CLOU received a TÜV Rheinland AS/NZS 3000 conformity report for Aqua-C at All Energy Australia, held in Melbourne on 28–29 October. The company’s 29 October release also disclosed the report. CLOU is seeking local distribution, EPC and developer partnerships. This technical and market-development milestone is not an Australian project approval, grid-connection permission or purchase order.

Wind

Nonowie proposes up to 126 turbines and optional storage
Source: RenewEconomy / Tilt Renewables | Date: 2026-10-30
URL: Original article · Supporting source
Summary: A 30 October report and Tilt’s project page describe the proposed Nonowie project near Whyalla: 1.2 GW of wind, up to 126 turbines and an optional 600 MW/4,800 MWh battery. It remains in development, planning and design—not approved or under construction. The reported rating of around 9.5 MW per turbine is a media inference, not a confirmed model or procurement specification.

Companies and projects

Neoen secures A$1.8 billion portfolio debt financing
Source: Neoen / pv magazine Australia | Date: 2026-10-30
URL: Original article · Supporting source
Summary: Neoen announced A$1.8 billion in debt financing from 13 lenders on 30 October for five operating or under-construction assets totalling around 1.2 GW: Hornsdale battery, Goyder South 1A wind, Culcairn solar and battery, and Narrogin wind. This is a portfolio financing milestone, not a claim that all five assets started construction or entered operation that day.

Grid and related infrastructure

Data-centre forecast lifts 2030 operational capacity to 3.9 GW
Source: Data Centres Australia / RenewEconomy | Date: 2026-10-30
URL: Original article · Supporting source
Summary: Data Centres Australia and DC Byte’s 30 October update tracks 163 operational facilities totalling 1.6 GW. It forecasts 3.9 GW by 2030, with 97 additional facilities, up from April’s 3.2 GW forecast. The viability-based forecast is not a guaranteed commissioning schedule or measured continuous load. It informs clean-supply and network planning; the separate proposed contracting rule covered on 29 October is not repeated.
