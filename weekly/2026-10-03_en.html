Tasmania conditionally commits up to A$12.5 million to Westbury BioHub
Source: Premier of Tasmania | Date: 2026-10-03
URL: Original article
Key point: Tasmania announced conditional support of up to A$12.5 million for Solstice Energy Tasmania’s proposed Westbury BioHub. The Valley Central Industrial Precinct project aims to convert up to 36,000 tonnes of organic waste into renewable gas and fertiliser. No funding has yet been provided and conditions remain.
ABC examines delivery risks behind AEMO’s reliability forecasts
Source: ABC News | Date: 2026-10-03
URL: Original article
Key point: ABC reported analysts’ concerns that anticipated generation and storage projects in AEMO’s reliability outlook may arrive late, potentially understating risk. AEMO’s 2026 ESOO stresses continued investment and timely delivery. This is a debate about forecast assumptions, not confirmation of an actual electricity shortfall.

MacIntyre delays prompt offtake changes; commercial operation targeted for mid-2027
Source: RenewEconomy | Date: 2026-10-03
URL: Original article
Key point: RenewEconomy reported that CleanCo increased its MacIntyre contract from 400 MW to 490 MW and extended its term, while Stanwell was discussing revisions. Acciona said more than 670 MW had been energised and targeted commercial operation in mid-2027. Energisation and commissioning are distinct from full commercial operation.

Snowy 2.0 cost reassessment remains unpublished a year after launch
Source: RenewEconomy | Date: 2026-10-03
URL: Original article
Key point: RenewEconomy reported that Snowy 2.0’s cost reassessment remained unpublished one year after its 3 October 2025 launch, which envisaged up to nine months.
