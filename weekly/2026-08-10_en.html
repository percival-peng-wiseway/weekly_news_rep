Policy & Regulation

Federal government launches Safeguard Mechanism review, including consideration of offset caps
ESD News · 2026-08-10 · Source
Key point: The Albanese Government has launched a review of the Safeguard Mechanism that will consider capping emissions offsets and focus on domestic abatement, industrial decarbonisation, and energy security through electrification and Australian renewables. Feedback closes on 18 September 2026, with completion scheduled for early 2027.

Solar

Aldi expands residential solar-and-battery packages to Perth and Adelaide
pv magazine Australia · 2026-08-10 · Source
Key point: Aldi, working with Sydney-based Tempo Group, has made its residential solar and battery packages available in selected Perth and Adelaide suburbs. Packages offer 7 kW or 7.9 kW solar, a 6 kW hybrid inverter, and 5–20 kWh batteries; the 7 kW solar-plus-5 kWh lithium iron phosphate battery package starts at AUD 7,499.
Engie commissions Victoria’s largest fully operational solar farm
pv magazine Australia · 2026-08-10 · Source
Key point: Engie has fully commissioned the 250 MW Goorambat East Solar Farm near Benalla in north-east Victoria, which comprises about 500,000 panels across 630 hectares. It is Engie’s first large-scale Australian solar project and Victoria’s largest fully operational solar facility.

Battery Storage (BESS)

Sydney’s Homebush 200 MW/431 MWh big battery receives development consent
RenewEconomy · 2026-08-10 · Source
Key point: The NSW Independent Planning Commission approved Ausgrid’s AUD 215 million, 200 MW/431 MWh battery beside a Homebush substation, about 13 km from Sydney CBD. The commission found it would support renewable integration and that fire and other risks could be managed through consent conditions; Ausgrid hopes to begin construction this year.

Wind

80-metre turbine blades begin 400 km transport to NSW’s Uungula Wind Farm
RenewEconomy · 2026-08-10 · Source
Key point: Eighty-metre turbine blades have begun travelling about 400 km from the Port of Newcastle to the Uungula Wind Farm under construction near Wellington in NSW’s Central West Orana. Developer Squadron Energy had previously reported the 414 MW project was around one-third complete.
