Policy & Regulation

Australia endorses global goal of 300 million solar homes by 2030
Source: Smart Energy Council | Date: 2026-10-08
URL：Original article
Key point: On 8 October at Pacific Pre-COP in Fiji, Chris Bowen endorsed the Global Rooftop Solar Declaration, which aims to double solar-equipped homes worldwide from about 150 million to 300 million by 2030. Priorities include simpler permits, household finance, grid upgrades and workforce training. This is an international policy endorsement, not a new Australian rebate or domestic installation mandate.

Solar

South Australia and Victoria set rooftop solar output records
Source: RenewEconomy | Date: 2026-10-08
URL：Original article
Key point: RenewEconomy reported on 8 October that rooftop solar reached 2,070 MW in South Australia at 12:30 pm and 4,191 MW in Victoria at midday on 7 October. These are instantaneous power records, not daily energy totals. The report attributes the estimates to Global Power Energy and notes that behind-the-meter battery activity remains incompletely visible.
AGL and Bega plan 1.23 MW rooftop solar under a 15-year PPA
Source: pv magazine Australia / AGL | Date: 2026-10-08
URL：Original article
Key point: In 8 October coverage of AGL’s 6 October announcement, Bega’s Wetherill Park facility in western Sydney is to receive a 1.23 MW rooftop system with more than 2,400 panels. AGL will build, own and operate it under a 15-year power purchase agreement. The forecast 9% first-year emissions reduction concerns the facility’s electricity use; no commissioning date was verified.

Battery Storage (BESS)

Enerven appointed to deliver 400 MW / 1,600 MWh Teebar battery
Source: Enerven / pv magazine Australia | Date: 2026-10-08
URL：Original article
Key point: Enerven’s 8 October release confirms its appointment by Atmos Renewables for Teebar, about 60 km west of Maryborough, Queensland. Valo will supply the battery technology; Enerven’s scope includes integration and a 275 kV substation. Mobilisation is expected in October, a start ceremony is scheduled for 20 October, and commercial operation is targeted for late 2028. This advances the delivery programme following previously reported financial close.
Aula seeks approval for 365 MW / 1.8 GWh Wellington North battery
Source: RenewEconomy / pv magazine Australia | Date: 2026-10-08
URL：Original article
Key point: RenewEconomy’s 8 October report details Aula Energy’s proposed battery at the operating 330 MW Wellington North solar farm in NSW. The approximately five-hour system represents around A$350 million of additional investment and requires a modification to the existing development consent. The proposal was already reported on 6 October; today’s coverage is not a new approval, construction start or commissioning.

Wind

Ballyrogan proposal scales back to 690 MW and up to 115 turbines
Source: RenewEconomy / Squadron Energy | Date: 2026-10-08
URL：Original article
Key point: RenewEconomy’s 8 October coverage describes Squadron Energy’s federal environmental referral for Ballyrogan near Ararat, Victoria. The current design is 690 MW with up to 115 turbines and a roughly 45 km, 220 kV overhead connection to the Western Renewables Link. Squadron’s September update already disclosed referral lodgements; the project remains in planning, with approval and construction still ahead.

Company & Project Developments

Australian and Pacific industry bodies launch solar mentoring programme
Source: Smart Energy Council | Date: 2026-10-08
URL：Original article
Key point: On 8 October, the Smart Energy Council and the Sustainable Energy Industry Association for Pacific Islands launched an Australia-Pacific Solar Mentoring Program in Fiji, with Australian Government in-principle support. Australian mentors will assist Pacific solar and battery installers through technical support and training. Local visits, installations and workshops depend on funding; the launch is an industry-capability partnership, not a confirmed government funding award.

Infrastructure & Transmission

HumeLink advances aerial stringing across its 365 km route
Source: pv magazine Australia / Transgrid | Date: 2026-10-08
URL：Original article
Key point: In 8 October reporting, Infravision drones and Leading Edge Helicopters are installing draw wires for HumeLink’s western and eastern sections. The 500 kV project involves around 9,000 km of conductor across a 365 km route, with more than 200 towers erected and completion targeted for late 2027. Stringing began earlier in 2026; this is a construction progress update, not energisation.
