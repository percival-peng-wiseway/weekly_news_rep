Policy & Regulation

Australia expands solar rebate eligibility to systems up to 1 MW
Source: Australian Government, Minister for Climate Change and Energy | Date: 2026-10-01
URL: Original article
Key point: The expanded Small-scale Renewable Energy Scheme takes effect for eligible solar systems installed from 1 October, lifting the maximum size from 100 kW to 1 MW. The government estimates an upfront cost reduction of around 20%. The Clean Energy Regulator says applications are expected to open in mid to late November, with eligible installations from 1 October able to apply then.
Baldon wind-farm hearing moved online
Source: NSW Independent Planning Commission | Date: 2026-10-01
URL: Original article
Key point: An IPC update dated 1 October moves the Baldon Wind Farm public hearing online. The case page lists the hearing for 1pm on 8 October and written submissions closing at 11:59pm on 14 October. The proposal remains under assessment; the procedural update is not project approval.

Solar

Victoria launches opt-in three-hour Midday Power Saver electricity offer
Source: RenewEconomy | Date: 2026-10-01
URL: Original article
Key point: Victoria's Midday Power Saver starts on 1 October, offering eligible households free electricity from 11am to 2pm daily after opting in. Customers need a smart meter and a qualifying retailer. Official guidance warns that rates outside the free window may be higher than on other plans, so savings depend on usage patterns. The state government specifies a daily free-usage limit of 24 kWh.
UNSW details A$18 million outdoor test of next-generation solar modules
Source: UNSW Sydney | Date: 2026-10-01
URL: Original article
Key point: UNSW outlined a five-year, A$18 million project to independently test commercial perovskite and perovskite-silicon tandem modules outdoors at Manly Vale. Supported by A$5.6 million from ARENA, the study will test up to 160 modules and examine durability and failure mechanisms under Australian conditions through 2031. It evaluates commercial readiness rather than claiming proven long-term performance.
Community Power Agency expands support for community-led solar projects
Source: Community Power Agency, public release | Date: 2026-10-01
URL: Original article
Key point: Community Power Agency said NSW funding will expand training and mentoring through its Community Energy Incubator, helping local solar proposals move from early feasibility toward investment readiness. The agency is one of 12 organisations sharing A$4.7 million under the Community Energy Activation Program. The broader grant awards were already announced by NSW on 8 September.

Wind

Origin withdraws from proposed 870 MW Skye Ridge wind farm
Source: RenewEconomy, reporting an Origin Energy statement | Date: 2026-10-01
URL: Original article
Key point: Origin told RenewEconomy it has dropped the proposed 870 MW Skye Ridge Wind Farm near Walcha, citing higher wind-generation construction costs and a reduced case for progressing two projects in the same region. It will concentrate capital and development resources on its Northern Tablelands Wind Farm. This is a development withdrawal, not closure of an operating wind farm.
All 83 turbines installed at Queensland’s 506 MW Wambo wind farm
Source: RenewEconomy, quoting Vestas and Aurora | Date: 2026-10-01
URL: Original article
Key point: RenewEconomy reported that all 83 turbines across the two-stage Wambo Wind Farm have been installed, including the 41 turbines in its 254 MW second stage. The 506 MW project is owned by Stanwell and Cubico. This is an installation milestone; the report does not establish that all of stage two has completed commissioning or entered full commercial operation.
NSW grants conditional consent for Bullawah wind-and-battery project
Source: NSW Independent Planning Commission | Date: 2026-10-01
URL: Original article
Key point: The NSW Independent Planning Commission approved BayWa r.e.’s Bullawah project on 1 October: up to 141 turbines, 804 MW of wind and a 359 MW / 718 MWh battery. Conditions cover roads, traffic, threatened-species protection, a battery fire-safety study and emergency planning. Consent is not financial close or operation.

Company & Project Developments

Ampol agrees to acquire Evie Networks for A$225 million
Source: Ampol | Date: 2026-10-01
URL: Original article
Key point: Ampol Energy signed an agreement to buy all of Evie Networks for A$225 million. If completed, it would add more than 1,030 charging bays and create a combined network of about 1,425 bays across over 400 sites. The deal requires ACCC clearance and is expected to complete in the first half of 2027; the businesses remain independent until completion.
Wärtsilä and RCT complete Valo energy-storage joint venture
Source: Wärtsilä | Date: 2026-10-01
URL: Original article
Key point: Wärtsilä announced completion of its energy-storage joint venture with RCT Solutions on 1 October, with each owning 50% of Valo. The independent storage integrator operates in Australia and other regions. Its global legacy portfolio exceeding 20 GWh is not newly added Australian capacity.

Infrastructure & Transmission

Project EnergyConnect enters phased operational testing across three NEM regions
Source: Australian Energy Market Operator (AEMO) | Date: 2026-10-01
URL: Original article
Key point: AEMO says operational testing and integration of the approximately 900 km Project EnergyConnect interconnector linking South Australia, Victoria and New South Wales are underway. It plans to progressively bring the link into operation from 1 October, increasing transfers as testing conditions permit, with testing expected through the end of 2027. This is phased commissioning, not a declaration of full-capacity operation.
