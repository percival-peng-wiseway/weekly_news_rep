Policy & Regulation

Australia expands solar rebate eligibility to systems up to 1 MW
Source: Australian Government, Minister for Climate Change and Energy | Date: 2026-10-01
URL: Original article
Key point: The expanded Small-scale Renewable Energy Scheme takes effect for eligible solar systems installed from 1 October, lifting the maximum size from 100 kW to 1 MW. The government estimates an upfront cost reduction of around 20%. The Clean Energy Regulator says applications are expected to open in mid to late November, with eligible installations from 1 October able to apply then.
Victoria launches opt-in three-hour Midday Power Saver electricity offer
Source: RenewEconomy | Date: 2026-10-01
URL: Original article
Key point: Victoria's Midday Power Saver starts on 1 October, offering eligible households free electricity from 11am to 2pm daily after opting in. Customers need a smart meter and a qualifying retailer. Official guidance warns that rates outside the free window may be higher than on other plans, so savings depend on usage patterns. The state government specifies a daily free-usage limit of 24 kWh.

Infrastructure & Transmission

Project EnergyConnect enters phased operational testing across three NEM regions
Source: Australian Energy Market Operator (AEMO) | Date: 2026-10-01
URL: Original article
Key point: AEMO says the approximately 900 km Project EnergyConnect interconnector linking South Australia, Victoria and New South Wales has entered operational testing. Transfers will increase progressively as testing conditions permit, with full commissioning expected by the end of 2027. Delivered by ElectraNet and Transgrid, the project adds a transmission pathway for renewable generation and storage across the three regions.
