Policy & Regulation

CEIG seeks financeable contracts and firmer transmission delivery under NEM reforms
Source: Clean Energy Investor Group (CEIG) | Date: 2026-10-14
URL：Original article
Key point: On 14 October, CEIG backed the proposed Electricity Services Entry Mechanism, while seeking centrally managed force-majeure risk, a zero-dollar price floor and limits on extreme spot-price exposure. It also called for two years’ notice before state procurement targets are reduced and stronger accountability for transmission delivery. These are investor-group recommendations, not adopted rules.
EnergyCo launches NSW renewable-energy jobs guide
Source: EnergyCo NSW | Date: 2026-10-14
URL：Original article
Key point: EnergyCo launched the NSW Renewable Energy Jobs Guide on 14 October at the Women in Energy and Renewables Summit. It sets out occupations, skills, qualifications and pathways across planning, construction, operation and maintenance, including supply-chain roles in logistics and shipping. The announcement launches a careers resource rather than a new project or hiring commitment.

Solar

Mowanjum’s 270 kW rooftop rollout shares solar credits across the community
Source: pv magazine Australia / Horizon Power | Date: 2026-10-14
URL：Original article
Key point: Reporting published on 14 October details Mechanical Project Services’ completed 270 kW installation across 27 rooftops at Mowanjum in WA’s Kimberley. Horizon Power’s Kimberley Communities Solar Saver programme shares solar credits among households, including those without panels. The actual switch-on date is not verified as 14 October; Horizon’s programme page had already scheduled initial credits for late August or early September.
East Fremantle completes municipal solar installations; park connection due in 2027
Source: Town of East Fremantle | Date: 2026-10-14
URL：Original article
Key point: The Town of East Fremantle announced on 14 October that solar and batteries had been installed at its Town Hall and Operations Depot, with solar also installed at Dovenby House. The A$796,000 programme includes solar and batteries at East Fremantle Community Park, which are expected to operate in 2027 after Western Power completes the connection. Installation completion does not mean every site is already operating.

Battery Storage (BESS)

Summerfield commissioning update points to operations in 2027
Source: pv magazine Australia / Summerfield BESS | Date: 2026-10-14
URL：Original article
Key point: On 14 October, pv magazine reported that Intera Renewables’ 240 MW / 960 MWh Summerfield battery near Mannum, South Australia, was undergoing testing and commissioning, with full operations expected in 2027. The developer’s 22 May update had already described final commissioning underway, so the October report should not be read as the first start of commissioning. The battery is not yet reported as commercially operational.
AEMO describes batteries’ growing contribution to peak supply and lower prices
Source: RenewEconomy / AEMO | Date: 2026-10-14
URL：Original article
Key point: RenewEconomy’s 14 October report covers AEMO chief Daniel Westerman’s 13 October speech: grid-scale batteries supplied an average 8% of demand during the 4–9 pm peak in the September quarter, while wholesale prices fell across all NEM regions. He also said homes with batteries drew 70–75% less peak-time grid electricity than homes with rooftop solar alone. These are reported system observations; the full quarterly report was still due the following week.

Wind

Origin reports Yanco Delta progress towards FID, with economics still challenging
Source: RenewEconomy / Origin Energy AGM | Date: 2026-10-14
URL：Original article
Key point: At Origin’s 14 October AGM, chief executive Frank Calabria said the proposed 1.5 GW Yanco Delta wind farm had progressed towards a final investment decision, but economics remained challenging despite Capacity Investment Scheme support. This was a development update, not an FID or construction announcement. Origin also reaffirmed its planning assumption that Eraring will close in April 2029.

Company & Project Developments

Banjima and Yindjibarndi sign exploratory renewables and transmission MOU
Source: Yindjibarndi Energy Corporation | Date: 2026-10-14
URL：Original article
Key point: A 14 October release announced an exclusive memorandum of understanding between Banjima and Yindjibarndi organisations, through Banjima Pty Ltd and Yindjibarndi Energy Corporation, to investigate renewable-energy and transmission opportunities on Banjima Country in the Pilbara. The agreement establishes an exploratory partnership; it does not commit either party to a specific project or investment decision.

Infrastructure & Transmission

ABB warns synchronous-condenser delivery can take five years
Source: RenewEconomy / ABB Australia | Date: 2026-10-14
URL：Original article
Key point: In a 14 October interview report, ABB Australia’s Aaron Trueman said a new synchronous condenser could take up to five years from order to delivery, with balance-of-plant installation requiring additional time. His assessment highlights procurement pressure on equipment supporting grid stability as coal generation retires. It is a supplier lead-time estimate, not a new equipment order or confirmed project delay.
