Solar

Transformer delivery advances New England Solar Stage 2
Source: RenewEconomy / ACEN Australia | Date: 2026-10-17
URL：Original article
Key point: Reporting on 17 October covers ACEN’s 8 October announcement that a 244-tonne transformer had reached the New England Solar substation in NSW after a 2,165 km journey, with installation under way. It supports the 320 MW second stage, taking planned total solar capacity to 720 MW. This is an equipment-delivery and installation milestone, not commissioning of Stage 2.

Battery Storage (BESS)

Amber reports A$2 million for customers during NSW price spike
Source: ABC News | Date: 2026-10-17
URL：Original article
Key point: ABC’s 17 October report on Monday 12 October’s NSW price spike quotes Amber saying around 30,000 customers earned A$2 million in two hours. AEMO told ABC that approximately 2,400 MW of NSW coal capacity was unavailable and demand rose nearly 2,000 MW in an hour; the report also highlights transmission constraints and household-battery coordination challenges. The company-reported revenue is specific to that episode, not recurring income or profit after all costs.

Company & Project Developments

Atmos acquires operating solar assets and wind development from BayWa r.e.
Source: RenewEconomy / Atmos Renewables | Date: 2026-10-17
URL：Original article
Key point: Reporting on 17 October follows Atmos’s 16 October announcement of a completed acquisition, including the operating 106 MWp Yatpool solar farm in Victoria, 20 MWp Hughenden in Queensland, and Bullawah and other development projects. Bullawah’s South West REZ access rights cover a 262 MW Stage 2 component, rather than its entire approximately 800 MW wind design. Atmos targets investment readiness in 2027, subject to remaining approvals and a final investment decision.
