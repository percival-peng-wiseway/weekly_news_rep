Battery storage

AEMO reclassifies Woreen battery and transmission-line trip risk
AEMO · 2026-10-31 · 145897 / 145902
Initial notice · Follow-up notice
AEMO notice 145897 records the Hazelwood–Rowville No.1 220 kV line and Woreen BESS tripping at 03:30 NEM time (AEST), or 04:30 Melbourne time (AEDT), on 31 October. AEMO did not instruct load shedding and had received no advice of bulk electrical load disconnection. Later notice 145902, issued at 08:17 AEST, says the cause had been identified but does not disclose it. As AEMO was not satisfied that recurrence was unlikely, it reclassified the event as a credible contingency from 08:00 AEST (09:00 AEDT) until further notice; no constraint set was invoked. This is an operating-security update, not a commissioning milestone. The notices do not establish the battery’s restoration time or justify a claim of widespread customer outages.
