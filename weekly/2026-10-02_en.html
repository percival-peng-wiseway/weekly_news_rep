Victoria's VNI West transmission project opens environmental documentation for public exhibition
RenewEconomy · 2026-10-02 · Source
Key point: VicGrid will publicly exhibit the Environment Effects Statement (EES) and draft planning scheme amendment for VNI West from 5 October to 16 November for review and submissions. The project proposes a 240 km double-circuit high-voltage line connecting the Victorian and NSW grids, with a 70 m easement; its estimated cost has risen from AUD 3.6 billion to AUD 7.6 billion.

Solar

Atlassian signs a 10-year virtual PPA backing Goulburn community solar-and-storage project
RenewEconomy · 2026-10-02 · Source
Key point: Atlassian has signed a 10-year virtual power purchase agreement with the Goulburn Community Energy Co-operative in NSW's Southern Tablelands, underwriting almost 70% of the project's output over the decade for its Sydney headquarters planned to open in 2028. The 2.2-hectare solar-and-storage project has 2,300 panels and a 4MWh battery, and connected to the grid in March 2026.
AUD 122 million Mutitjulu utilities upgrade includes 1MW solar and 3MWh battery
pv magazine Australia · 2026-10-02 · Source
Key point: The Mutitjulu community in Uluru-Kata Tjuta National Park, Northern Territory, will receive a 1MW solar array and 3MWh battery energy storage system under a federal AUD 122 million utilities upgrade, reducing diesel dependence. Works are scheduled to start in late October 2026 and finish by the end of 2028; the contract has been awarded to South Australian contractor Exact Contracting.

Battery Storage (BESS)

Atmos reaches financial close for Queensland's 400MW/1,600MWh Teebar BESS
RenewEconomy · 2026-10-02 · Source
Key point: Atmos Renewables says its Capacity Investment Scheme-backed Teebar BESS in Queensland has reached financial close at 400MW/1,600MWh; it will use Valo technology and be built by Enerven. The company says it will retain ownership and operate the asset through construction and after commissioning.

Wind

Western Australia's 470MW Parron Maam Marang wind project reaches financial close and proceeds to construction
RenewEconomy · 2026-10-02 · Source
Key point: Atmos Renewables' 470MW Parron Maam Marang wind project in the Wheatbelt, about 200 km north of Perth, has reached financial close and will proceed to construction, supported by a power purchase agreement with state-owned retailer Synergy. The project also has federal Capacity Investment Scheme support; Atmos will retain ownership and operate it once completed.

Ampol plans AUD 225 million acquisition of Evie Networks, adding more than 1,030 charging bays
pv magazine Australia · 2026-10-02 · Source
Key point: Ampol Energy has signed a share-sale agreement to acquire 100% of Fast Cities Australia, trading as Evie Networks, for AUD 225 million, subject to Australian Competition and Consumer Commission clearance.
