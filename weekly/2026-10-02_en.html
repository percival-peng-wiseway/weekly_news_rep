Policy & Regulation

VNI West environmental assessment opens for submissions on 5 October
Source: VicGrid | Date: 2026-10-02
URL: Original article
Key point: VicGrid announced a six-week public exhibition of VNI West’s Environment Effects Statement and draft Planning Scheme Amendment, from 5 October to 11:59 pm on 16 November. Submissions will inform the Planning Minister’s assessment. This is an assessment milestone, not construction approval.
South Australia delays upfront gas-connection charging reform
Source: RenewEconomy | Date: 2026-10-02
URL: Original article
Key point: RenewEconomy reported that South Australia delayed the reform requiring new gas customers to pay connection costs upfront, pending a later ministerial commencement notice. The national rule started on 1 October in applicable NSW, ACT and Queensland networks, aiming to prevent new-connection costs being spread across existing customers.

Solar

Atlassian signs ten-year virtual PPA with Goulburn community solar project
Source: AAP via RenewEconomy | Date: 2026-10-02
URL: Original article
Key point: Atlassian announced a ten-year virtual power purchase agreement covering almost 70% of Goulburn Community Energy Co-operative’s output, supporting its Sydney headquarters planned for 2028. The solar-and-storage project, with 2,300 panels and a 4 MWh battery, connected in March 2026; this update concerns offtake.

Battery Storage (BESS)

Atmos reaches financial close on 400 MW / 1,600 MWh Teebar battery
Source: Atmos Renewables | Date: 2026-10-02
URL: Original article
Key point: Atmos and Igneo announced financial close for the CIS-supported 400 MW / 1,600 MWh Teebar BESS beside Powerlink’s Teebar Creek substation in Queensland. Atmos will retain ownership and operate the four-hour battery once completed. Financial close does not mean it is operating.
Amber reports September V2X results from 64 connected chargers
Source: Amber | Date: 2026-10-02
URL: Original article
Key point: Amber’s 2 October fleet update reports 8,780 kWh discharged during September across 64 connected chargers: 5,096 kWh to homes and 3,684 kWh to the grid. The company estimates A$1,605 in avoided imports and export credits, about A$25 per site; this is not net profit after all costs.

Wind

470 MW Parron Maam Marang wind farm reaches financial close
Source: Atmos Renewables | Date: 2026-10-02
URL: Original article
Key point: Atmos and Igneo announced financial close for the 470 MW Parron Maam Marang wind farm in Western Australia’s Wheatbelt. The CIS-backed project has a long-term PPA with Synergy. Atmos will retain ownership through construction and operate it after completion.

Company & Project Developments

RedEarth battery business enters voluntary administration amid cash-flow pressure
Source: RenewEconomy | Date: 2026-10-02
URL: Original article
Key point: RenewEconomy reported on 2 October that RedEarth Energy Storage appointed B&T Advisory’s Travis Pullen as administrator on 25 September, with a first creditors’ meeting scheduled for 8 October. A board letter cited in the reporting attributed cash-flow pressure partly to cheaper imported batteries; that explanation is the company’s account.
Volt expands into community solar-and-storage assets with equity crowdfunding
Source: pv magazine Australia | Date: 2026-10-02
URL: Original article
Key point: pv magazine reported that Australian solar-tile company Volt expanded into developing, owning and operating energy assets and opened an equity crowdfunding offer. Its OnMarket offer page describes Volt Projects’ community-scale solar-and-battery focus. An open offer is not completed fundraising or commissioned infrastructure.

Infrastructure & Transmission

Marinus Link formally starts major works at Burnie
Source: Australian Government, Minister for Climate Change and Energy | Date: 2026-10-02
URL: Original article
Key point: At the 2 October Burnie press conference, the federal energy minister formally announced the start of major works on Marinus Link. The planned 750 MW bidirectional Tasmania–Victoria connection forms part of Project Marinus alongside the North West Transmission Developments. Several years of construction remain before operation.
Federal quantum grants back grid forecasting and timing demonstrators
Source: Australian Government, Department of Industry ministers | Date: 2026-10-02
URL: Original article
Key point: Federal demonstrator funding includes A$3.6 million for SQC, Schneider Electric and UNSW to develop quantum-enhanced AI for energy forecasting and renewable integration, and A$2.2 million for QuantX Labs to test grid-timing technology independent of external signals. These are demonstrators, not completed commercial deployments.
